# advisor-common-factors — core principles, tier-ranked by evidence

The lens's one claim: people talk themselves into and out of change, so the operative variable
is **whose voice says the reasons**. Ambivalence is the normal state; the ratio of change talk
to sustain talk tracks what happens next; and a helper can move that ratio with observable,
countable behaviours. Everything below is sorted by how well that chain is actually measured —
not by how central it feels to the method.

Two standing caveats apply to every tier. First, there is no trial of the 4th-edition book and
no trial of most individual moves: the evidence is package-level trials plus process research on
language, and package effects do not transfer automatically to any one micro-move. Second, the
delivery-mode indirectness the guidelines lens fixes applies here in full — no study tested a
clinician-free format, so procedures transfer to solo use but effect sizes do not.

---

## Tier A — Directly measured procedures

Few, and none of them is the "essence" of the method.

### Personalized feedback with the decision left to the person (direct trials)
The check-up format — assess, then return results with no strings attached — has its own trials,
including a computer-delivered version; participants roughly halved their drinking, largely
without entering treatment. It failed at the purpose it was designed for (pushing people into
treatment), which is the origin of the false claim that feedback does not work. Related: norm
correction changes behaviour when someone's sense of "normal" rests on an outlier peer group.
Moves resting on this: the ask–offer–ask envelope around any feedback [CF:AOA].

### Training: workshop attendance does not produce the skill
Post-workshop residual gain was about **one extra reflection per 10 minutes at 4 months**, with
**no change in client responses**. Only feedback *plus* coaching shifted client language; reaching
trial-delivery competence took on average five 30-minute coaching calls. Self-reported skill errs
upward. This is the best-measured negative finding in the tradition and the reason [CF:PRACTICE-LOOP]
is counting rather than self-rating.

### Values-based single-session work (one trial, one target)
A single-session values-based MI trial reduced alcohol use at 3 and 6 months, with self-esteem as
the mediator — the empirical footing under [CF:VALUE-GAP], and the only outcome trial in the values
family. The card sort itself [CF:VALUES] has none.

## Tier B — Method-level package evidence (small-to-medium, heterogeneous)

### The package: 2,000+ controlled trials, ~25% null
Effects are typically small-to-medium with wide between-study variance; roughly a quarter of trials
are null, and multisite trials show the method working at some sites and not others. Addiction is the
strongest domain (25+ reviews). There is little support for the method alone where the target is
opioid or stimulant use. What this licenses: the *stance and skill set* as a default way of talking
about change [CF:GUIDE], [CF:MIRROR], [CF:OPENQ], [CF:AFFIRM] — not a claim of superiority over
any specific active alternative.

### Evoking is what separates this from generic empathic listening
Trials of evoking skills raise the change-talk ratio, and training that under-teaches the directional
component reduces efficacy. This is the strongest structural claim the lens can make: [CF:DIRQ],
[CF:RULER], [CF:OARS-CT], [CF:BOUQUET] are not decoration on top of listening.

### Information exchange and permission
Trials on information exchange plus group-format data support the ask–offer–ask cycle [CF:AOA] and
the permission gate [CF:ASK-PERMISSION]; the coding systems treat persuasion without permission and
confrontation as the two explicitly method-inconsistent behaviours. Brief empathic advice from a
credible source can itself trigger change — which is why the ban is on unpermissioned advice, not
on advice.

### Mandated and low-motivation entry
Package-level trials in mandated settings support [CF:WILLING-STEP]; matching data suggest larger
benefit at *lower* initial motivation or higher anger. Read as a hypothesis about who benefits, not
as an assignment rule — the guidelines lens rates algorithmic matching as unvalidated.

## Tier C — Process research: correlational, sturdy, not directly steerable

### Change talk / sustain talk sequencing
Sequential studies show what the helper reflects shapes what the person says next [CF:DIRREF],
[CF:DOUBLESIDE], and that the amount of **sustain talk** predicts outcome better than the amount of
change talk [CF:DISCORD-SCAN]. Coding of the seven change-talk categories [CF:EAR] is the measurement
backbone of forty years of this research. All seven types favour subsequent change; a declared
intention is not required [CF:COMMIT-LISTEN].

### Alliance and empathy — association, ceiling ≈8% of variance
Alliance correlates with outcome at **r ≈ .278** (about 8% of variance) and is rated LOW after the
guidelines lens removed an invalid upgrade; the older "alliance d=0.57 versus method d≤0.20" contrast
is retracted as non-commensurable (correlational versus randomized numbers). Weak alliance predicts
dropout (d ≈ .55). Empathy→outcome is likewise correlational. Treat alliance as a validator — is the
contact working — never as proof that what you do inside it is interchangeable [CF:RAPID-ENGAGE].

### Resistance is interactional, not a trait
In-session resistance rises and falls with what the helper just did; softening reduces it, refuting
strengthens it [CF:REFLECT-SUSTAIN]. This is observation plus coding, replicated but not experimental.

## Tier D — Author craft: one transcript, or an explicit "no trial isolates this"

Named as such by the authors themselves. Usable, but never cited as evidence:
amplified and understated reflection [CF:INTENSITY-DIAL]; coming alongside [CF:COMING-ALONGSIDE];
riding the pendulum to the sustain side; reframing; the readiness-sign list [CF:READY-SIGNS];
the discord repairs [CF:REPAIR-DISCORD]; the agenda bubble sheet [CF:AGENDA-MAP]; brief action
planning as a five-step package [CF:BAP]; seed-planting as a standalone strategy [CF:SEEDFRAME];
setback bounding [CF:SETBACK-NORMALIZE]. The ordering effect in double-sided reflections is
illustrated, not measured.

## Tier E — Extrapolation, borrowed, or unmeasured

- **Mechanism is under-determined.** Self-regulation, self-determination, client-centred theory,
  dissonance and self-perception each explain part of the findings; none covers them. "Relational or
  technical, which matters more" is treated as a malformed question, not answered.
- **The high-readiness contraindication** [CF:SKIPEVOKE] rests on several reports of adverse effects
  at high initial readiness; the causal direction is inferred, never tested. It is still the sharpest
  contraindication signal the tradition owns.
- **Autonomy framing** [CF:AUTONOMY-EMPHASIS] borrows from reactance research; the claim that tone
  inverts the technique is clinical assertion.
- **The miracle question and time-frame shifts** are borrowed from solution-focused work.
- **Cultural transfer.** The individual-autonomy framing is Western; in collectivist contexts the
  relevant "self" may be family or community — flagged by the authors, not resolved, and the whole
  surrounding corpus is Western with country a significant moderator. Younger children are largely
  unstudied; the authors' workaround is working through parents.

## Calibration inherited from the guidelines lens

Do not restate any of these in a stronger form than written here:

- Between-method differences ≈ zero holds for **depression only** (d = 0.01…−0.30 against
  d = −0.62…−0.92 versus waitlist). It does not extend to fear/avoidance-driven or ritual-driven
  targets, nor to schools absent from that network.
- "Common factors ≥ modality, with a short exception list" is **disputed**, not settled. This lens
  is a question repertoire, not proof that method choice is irrelevant.
- Allegiance inflates developer-run effects by roughly 30%; this tradition is not exempt.
- Homework presence is d = 0.48 at LOW; **no reliable completion benchmark exists**, so [CF:BAP]
  is judged on setup quality and on what changed in the episode, not on compliance.
- Adverse events occur in 5–20% of psychotherapy participants and 5–10% leave worse; temporary
  worsening is baseline, not a switch signal — but flooding past 24 hours, functional decline,
  intrusive images or dissociation are stop-and-route signals regardless of what this lens is doing.
- Grief: no protocol here applies to grief. Grief work has an entry condition — measured elevated
  prolonged-grief symptoms — and belongs to a live therapist. Nothing in this lens reframes a loss
  as an opportunity, and change-talk coding is not applied to grief [CF:EAR].

## What the tiering changes in practice

1. Lead with Tier B skills; they are the part with a package behind them.
2. Quote Tier A numbers only for the three items that own them — feedback, training, the values trial.
3. Use Tier D craft freely, label it as craft, and drop it the moment discord rises.
4. Never argue from alliance: ~8% ceiling, no steering wheel. What is steerable is the
   question/reflection ratio, the permission gate, and which strand you reflect.
