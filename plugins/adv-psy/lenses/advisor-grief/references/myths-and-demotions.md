# advisor-grief — myths, demotions, and documented failure modes

What this tradition has thrown out, what got demoted inside it, and how it hurts people when it is applied wrongly. Numbers here are in their post-correction form per PG26; the pre-correction versions must not be reused anywhere.

---

## 1. Refuted outright

**Stage models of mourning, including the five-stage sequence applied to grief.** Worden refutes them from inside the tradition, citing Maciejewski 2007: stages invite literalism, a fixed order, and self-blame for the stage one appears to have skipped. Replaced by tasks that can be worked ([GR:TASK-MAP]). No output of this lens uses stage language, in any paraphrase.

**Universal grief counselling.** Routine referral of all bereaved people shows no benefit (Parkes 1998). Most bereavement is self-limiting and a substantial resilient group needs nothing at all. The mirror myth — "grief counselling does not work, or harms" — is equally wrong as stated: it rests on weak studies plus a deterioration analysis that was statistically invalid (Larson & Hoyt 2007). Correct statement: it works for screened, indicated, or self-referring mourners, and approximately nothing happens otherwise.

**Grief-CBT superiority.** Demoted MODERATE→LOW and retracted in PG26. Hao 2024 network meta-analysis (55 studies, N=10,330) finds five modalities significant with overlapping intervals. Say: works at elevated grief symptoms, no winning brand. Never present this lens's procedures as beating another school's.

**"Everyone must do grief work, and everyone must make meaning."** 20–30% adapt well with no meaning search at all, and among those who do search, fewer than half find anything at a year (Davis 2000). Absence of visible affect is not by itself pathology. Continuous confrontation is not the adaptive pattern — oscillation is.

**Venting as progress.** Unfocused vehement emotion predicts worse outcomes a year on (Parkes 2001). Discharge is not integration.

**"Time heals."** Only what is done with the time does anything. Fixed deadlines — four weeks, four months, a year — set mourners up to blame themselves for being late, and self-blame is the strongest known determinant of outcome (Stroebe 2014). Deadlines are therefore not merely useless here; they load the one variable that matters.

**"Women fare better because they have more support."** Refuted (Stroebe 1999). Worse, the response to intervention is gender-crossed: men gained more from affect-focused work and women from problem-focused work (Schut 1997) — the useful move is training the non-dominant style, not confirming the stereotype. Fathers do grieve; they do it alone, in cars, at graves.

## 2. Demoted inside the tradition

**Worden's own evidence base.** The four tasks, the chair work, the linking object, the graded memory ordering — method canon, not outcome evidence. Where trials exist they are method-level analogues run by other people (Shear's 16-session treatment; writing-therapy trials). The lens must never present a Worden move as trial-proven.

**The self-confirming loop.** The empirical spine of the book is Worden's own Harvard studies, and his closing verdict that grief therapy works and its effectiveness is visible is author confidence. Calibrate to indicated-only d≈0.53, not to that sentence.

**ICG scores.** A snapshot of current symptoms, not a predictor of course. Used as a forecast, it manufactures both false alarm and false reassurance.

**Lazare's twelve clues.** Endorsed clinically, never validated as an instrument. Any single clue is meaningless on its own; possession-keeping in particular is uninterpretable without cultural and religious context.

**Bereavement-overload sequencing, attachment-style tailoring, technique selection generally.** No dismantling study exists in this tradition — which component of any package carries the effect (the retelling? the chair? the homework?) has never been isolated. Technique choice runs on clinical judgement, and clinical judgement about grief has not been benchmarked.

**Resilience is under-weighted throughout the book.** Bonanno's challenge — a large resilient majority, delayed grief mostly absent — is cited and then argued around rather than integrated. Grief work stays the default frame in a literature where the default outcome is coping without it.

**Dated diagnostic edge.** Written before the current prolonged-grief category and its measures; the medication guidance is old consensus. Anything about thresholds or scales comes from PG26, not from the book.

**Anglo-American clinic frame.** Culture appears as a mediator and as a caution, but the moves assume Western rituals, an office dyad, and verbal processing. Non-Western mourning gets anecdotes, not method. PG26's geographic bound applies: nothing here is validated outside Western samples.

## 3. Anti-recommendations — what not to do, and why

**Do not run a grief protocol on unremarkable scores.** At uncomplicated grief the effect is near zero and the intervention is potentially harmful. Measure first ([GR:MEASURE-FIRST]); no measurement, no protocol.

**Do not recommend medication for ordinary acute grief.** Slow, rarely touches ordinary grief, and questionably useful. Any medication question goes to a physician, full stop — this lens does not discuss drugs.

**Avoid the platitude set.** "I know how you feel", "be brave", "you still have your other children", "you can have another child" — these serve the speaker's discomfort and collude with denial. The one truthful reassurance is that the person will survive it.

**Avoid every positive reframe.** Growth framings, silver linings, "what this taught you", benefit-finding introduced from outside — pushed early, they invalidate the loss and skip the pain (Gamino & Sewell 2004). Meaning work is only ever supported when the person's own search is already running.

**Avoid popular grief literature organised around stages, timelines, or "letting go".** Stage sequences are refuted; decathexis framings contradict the continuing-bonds evidence; roughly 18% of self-help books have been found to carry harmful advice, and this genre is not exempt.

**Do not treat the resilient as under-reacting.** No visible collapse is a common and well-documented course, not suppression to be excavated.

## 4. Documented iatrogenic patterns

- **Surfacing anger and leaving it unresolved.** Anger raised and then turned inward can leave the person worse than before the work started — the specific failure of [GR:AMBIV-GRADE] done badly or done alone.
- **Chair work outside its contraindications.** Author-contraindicated where reality testing or identity is unstable; PG26 adds risk of retraumatisation, self-harm or suicidality, and notes the state gate before experiential work is not something anyone can apply to themselves.
- **Exposure-shaped work run solo.** A failed exposure reinforces the fear it was aimed at. Loss exposure and repeated retelling have no tested solo format ([GR:RETELL-DEATH], [GR:LETTER-UNFINISHED]).
- **Prompted detailed retelling of a fresh trauma.** Debriefing does not prevent posttraumatic outcomes and carries a harm signal (odds ratio around 2.5). After a violent death, stabilise first ([GR:TRAUMA-FIRST]).
- **Rapid replacement.** A quick remarriage can cut the grief off and add a second loss later; a quickly conceived next pregnancy carries the replacement-child pattern.
- **Irreversible decisions made to escape pain.** Selling the home, relocating, changing career — the harms are observational (Lopata 1996; Legg & Sherick 1976) but consistent.
- **Compelled ritual in either direction.** Forcing a child to attend a funeral, forcing an adult to view a body, or refusing someone the choice — all three produce the same class of damage.
- **Intervening with the child while the surviving parent is left unsupported.** Inverts the strongest finding in the lens.
- **Group entry too early or badly matched.** Under six weeks post-loss, or as the only person in the room with that kind of loss, the group makes things worse rather than better.
- **The reassurance loop.** Repeated returns with the same anxious question about the loss are not requests for information; supplying another round of reassurance reinforces the loop. Name it instead.
- **Endless availability with no closing.** A 2 a.m. spiral with no end ritual is the documented top harm mode of this format, and grief is exactly the material that produces 2 a.m.

## 5. Controversies left open

Delayed grief at 8% (Worden, two years) versus 4% (Bonanno, 18 months) on the same instruments. Forewarning helps (Parkes) versus no effect (Nielsen 2016 systematic review). Suicide grief inherently more intense (Farberow) versus not (Cleiren). Divorce after a child's death is not conclusively elevated. Expected stigma-rejection after a stigmatised death often fails to materialise, and the concealment undertaken to avoid it becomes the actual cost. Bereaved parents generally do want to talk about their child — the common belief runs backwards.
