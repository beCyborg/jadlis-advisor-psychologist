# advisor-psy-guidelines-2026 — moves

Calibration acts, not techniques. This lens does not deliver a method; it sets evidence weights, base rates, self-help contraindications, normative timelines, and the tier boundary the method lenses inherit. Tier is assigned per OPERATION (safety-protocol §3), never per school. Codes checked for uniqueness across the whole file.

## A. Choosing a lens and routing a target

### [PG26:TARGET-NOT-BRAND] Pick the lens by target, not by brand
**Move:** Choose a lens by problem-target and personal acceptability, and say out loud that claimed method superiority is not a selection criterion here.
**APPLY WHEN:** depressive-withdrawal and everyday-anxiety targets where behavioural, cognitive and interpersonal lenses are interchangeable. / **AVOID WHEN:** ritual/compulsion-like or trauma-memory targets; extending interchangeability to acceptance-based or schema lenses.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р1, MODERATE, depression only (Barth 2013, 198 RCTs/N=15,118; between-method d=0.01…−0.30).

### [PG26:PROTOCOL-TARGETS] Protocol-specific targets get protocol treatment
**Move:** Route ritual-and-avoidance targets and traumatic-memory targets out of the "any lens will do" pool and into protocol-specific treatment with a clinician.
**APPLY WHEN:** compulsion-like rituals, safety behaviours, intrusive traumatic memory. / **AVOID WHEN:** generic worry or low-mood targets — importing protocol machinery there adds burden without evidence.
**Tier:** REQUIRES-THERAPIST / **Evidence:** Р1, MODERATE — equivalence does not transfer to trauma or ritual targets.

### [PG26:ROUTING-AS-HYPOTHESES] Target→method arrows are hypotheses, not assignments
**Move:** Present every routing arrow (self-criticism→compassion work, perfectionism→perfectionism-specific cognitive work, withdrawal→activation, repeating interpersonal pattern→interpersonal lens plus schema map, "hard to leave a relationship"→skills lens) as a working hypothesis for which questions to ask.
**APPLY WHEN:** composing the lens set for a query. / **AVOID WHEN:** the arrow would be read as a treatment assignment or a claim of superiority.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р1 joint verifier verdict — roughly half the arrows rest on LOW.

### [PG26:NO-ALGO-MATCHING] No personalized-advantage matching
**Move:** Refuse to score a profile and assign a method from it; name the temptation instead and fall back to target plus acceptability.
**APPLY WHEN:** anyone proposes algorithmic method-to-profile matching, including "the council will compute the best modality for you". / **AVOID WHEN:** never appropriate as decision authority.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р1, VERY LOW — no prospective validation of matching indices.

### [PG26:SEQ-FIRST] Skills → map → depth; enter by cognitive style
**Move:** Order any program as regulation/skills first, cognitive-and-pattern map second, deep memory or parts work last; pick the entry lens by cognitive style (people who intellectualize enter through structured cognitive work; absent mental imagery means chair-style work instead of imagery).
**APPLY WHEN:** choosing a starting point or ordering a multi-step program. / **AVOID WHEN:** the user already sequenced this with their therapist; or imagery failure is about to be read as resistance rather than as absent imagery.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р2, LOW — community-derived process consensus, failure modes not prevalence.

### [PG26:INDIRECTNESS-TAX] Charge the two indirectness layers before quoting any effect
**Move:** Before attaching a number to a self-directed plan, subtract two layers: clinical→subclinical population, and live-therapist→AI delivery; state that procedures transfer while effect sizes do not.
**APPLY WHEN:** any effect size is about to be used to justify solo or AI-mediated work. / **AVOID WHEN:** the number is being used descriptively about live therapy and is labelled as such.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р1/Р3 added indirectness layer — no study tested a clinician-free format.

## B. Gates before experiential or exposure-shaped work

### [PG26:STATE-GATE] State gate before any experiential technique
**Move:** Gate on a stable sense of self plus intact reality testing before imagery, chair work, parts contact or exposure; route to a live session on any doubt, and treat parts language as an interface, not as literal entities.
**APPLY WHEN:** any experiential technique is proposed, including parts-work framings. / **AVOID WHEN:** never skip — the gate is not self-administrable; literalized parts work is specifically unsafe with psychosis-like states, severe dissociation or unstable identity.
**Tier:** REQUIRES-THERAPIST / **Evidence:** Р2, author-issued contraindications, HIGH for the contraindication itself.

### [PG26:EXPOSURE-THERAPIST] Hierarchy design and trauma reprocessing are therapist operations
**Move:** Stop council output at formulation plus target whenever the plan is exposure-shaped; hierarchy design, escalation, loss/rejection exposure and schema mode-work with vulnerable material go to the live session.
**APPLY WHEN:** any avoidance or fear work, any plan whose logic is "approach the feared thing". / **AVOID WHEN:** no exception; never improvise flooding, never design a hierarchy in chat.
**Tier:** REQUIRES-THERAPIST / **Evidence:** Р1 safety block + Р4 professional guideline (graded, repeated, prolonged exposure is the standard), MODERATE–HIGH.

### [PG26:ERP-NO-ESC] Support the agreed hierarchy, never escalate it
**Move:** Help execute and log a therapist-agreed exposure hierarchy — timing, logging, obstacle plan — and hold the difficulty level fixed between sessions.
**APPLY WHEN:** a hierarchy exists and was agreed in session. / **AVOID WHEN:** no agreed hierarchy exists, or the user pushes to raise difficulty solo — a failed exposure reinforces the fear.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р2, LOW — process consensus; the harm direction (failed solo exposure) is the better-supported half.

### [PG26:IMG-NONTRAUMA] Solo imagery rescripting only on non-traumatic targets
**Move:** Allow self-guided imagery rescripting only on non-traumatic images (for example anxious future scenarios), with mood monitoring before and after and a written stop rule.
**APPLY WHEN:** an intrusive image without a traumatic core. / **AVOID WHEN:** the target is a traumatic memory or a hotspot — early rescripting is therapist-performed even inside therapy (Arntz 2025).
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р2, author-issued contraindication HIGH; the permitted non-traumatic variant LOW.

### [PG26:NO-FRESH-DEBRIEF] No prompted retelling of recent trauma
**Move:** Refuse to elicit a detailed chronological retelling of a recent traumatic event in solo or AI work; note that the person mentioning the event spontaneously is a different thing from being walked through it.
**APPLY WHEN:** the event is recent, or distress is trauma-driven. / **AVOID WHEN:** confusing this with ordinary disclosure — the ban is on prompted structured retelling, not on the person speaking.
**Tier:** REQUIRES-THERAPIST / **Evidence:** Р4 Cochrane single-session debriefing, MODERATE; harm signal around OR 2.5.

### [PG26:GRIEF-GATE] Measure grief first; loss exposure only with a therapist
**Move:** Measure prolonged-grief symptoms and PHQ-9 before any grief-directed work; only confirmed elevated grief symptoms open therapist-led loss exposure plus a restorative component, described as a procedure rather than a brand.
**APPLY WHEN:** unfinished grief surfaces as a theme. / **AVOID WHEN:** scores are unremarkable — no grief protocol then; effects are near zero and the intervention can do harm in uncomplicated grief.
**Tier:** REQUIRES-THERAPIST (the measurement step is SELF) / **Evidence:** Р1 entry condition MODERATE; brand superiority LOW and retracted (Hao 2024 NMA, five modalities, overlapping CIs).

## C. Running a structured session and between-session practice

### [PG26:SKELETON] Fixed seven-step session skeleton
**Move:** Run any structured self-session on a fixed skeleton — check-in, bridge from last time, joint agenda of one to three items, review of the last practice, one topic, summary in the person's own words, one micro-task plus feedback.
**APPLY WHEN:** any structured self-session or AI-assisted session. / **AVOID WHEN:** opening with new material before the last practice is reviewed — that teaches the person the practice does not matter.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р4, MODERATE (training source plus client-experience studies).

### [PG26:MICRO-TASK] One micro-task; review quality; non-completion is a design failure
**Move:** Assign exactly one practice at at most one step and five minutes, started in-session, written down, with a stated rationale, a link to the goal and a plan B; review its quality rather than its completion, and on a miss walk the failure taxonomy — unclear goal, unclear instruction, unrealistic size, force majeure.
**APPLY WHEN:** every between-session interval, and every reported miss. / **AVOID WHEN:** any two-plus-step task (undoable by default), compliance checklists, or shaming a skip — the shame route is iatrogenic and there is no completion benchmark to shame against.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р4 setup quality MODERATE; Р3 homework presence d=0.48 LOW, quality over quantity g=1.07 vs 0.51 LOW.

### [PG26:WRITING-BOUND] Structured writing yes; unstructured venting during active rumination no
**Move:** Permit structured expressive writing as a practice, and block only unstructured venting while the person is in active rumination-driven distress.
**APPLY WHEN:** journaling or writing practice is proposed. / **AVOID WHEN:** repeating the blanket claim that expressive writing is unsafe — a newer meta-analysis finds a small positive effect.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р4 bounded correction, LOW–MODERATE.

### [PG26:SELFHELP-VETTING] Vet self-help material and add human guidance
**Move:** Treat unguided material as the weakest format available, add any human guidance that exists (a lay helper closes most of the gap), and screen a recommended book or app for harmful advice before naming it.
**APPLY WHEN:** any book, workbook or app is about to be recommended. / **AVOID WHEN:** presenting unguided self-help as equivalent to guided formats, or as harmful in itself — the IPD data show 5.8% vs 9.1% deterioration, i.e. not harmful overall.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р2/Р3 — unguided < guided HIGH; negative effects reported in 14–92% of self-help interventions and roughly 18% of self-help books carry harmful advice, MODERATE–LOW.

## D. Measurement, progress judgment, dose

### [PG26:TRIAD-ROM] Per-session triad; alerts fire scenarios, not graphs
**Move:** Measure a triad every session — a symptom scale (PHQ-9, GAD-7 or CORE-10), functioning, and a brief end-of-session alliance item, under ten items and five to ten minutes — and run two deterioration loops (reliable-change worsening; at least one SD off the expected trajectory), where every alert triggers a pre-written scenario ending in escalation to a human.
**APPLY WHEN:** any ongoing course or structured self-help with per-session data. / **AVOID WHEN:** measurement is rare or pre-post only (no effect then); alerts exist without an action protocol; or a large payoff is expected — the plan value is close to zero and the case for building it is cost, not effect.
**Tier:** SELF (the escalation branch is REQUIRES-THERAPIST) / **Evidence:** Р3, LOW — six independent meta-analyses give d≈0.13–0.27; off-track subgroup bounded toward zero with an independent scale.

### [PG26:JUDGE-PROCESS] Judge by episode change and competence, not by fidelity or alliance
**Move:** Evaluate progress by what changed in a concrete episode or behaviour plus delivery competence; use alliance only as a "is the contact working" validator.
**APPLY WHEN:** any progress review or session review. / **AVOID WHEN:** citing the alliance correlation (r=.278, about 8% of variance) as proof that method choice is irrelevant, or treating protocol fidelity as a quality measure.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р3 adherence-to-outcome null MODERATE (pre-registered meta-analysis, the strongest claim in that report); alliance LOW.

### [PG26:DOSE-DENSITY] No fixed session count; density is an acute-phase tool only
**Move:** Steer course length by the psychometric trajectory rather than a planned number of sessions, and in the acute phase prefer two sessions a week at the same total, framed as a way out of the acute phase.
**APPLY WHEN:** planning course length; acute-phase low mood with retention at risk. / **AVOID WHEN:** selling density as lasting superiority — the advantage is gone by 12–24 months and it is not cost-effective.
**Tier:** SELF (frequency changes are agreed in session) / **Evidence:** Р3 — dose LOW (4–26 sessions in routine practice, naturalistic mean under 5); 2×/week RCT N=200, acute d=0.55, MODERATE acute-only.

### [PG26:EARLY-RESPONSE] Read early response as a probability shift, not a verdict
**Move:** Treat early improvement as raising the odds of recovery about sixfold, and early non-improvement as still leaving roughly a quarter of people recovering by the end; use it to trigger a review, not a decision to quit.
**APPLY WHEN:** the two-to-four-week review, and whenever someone concludes "it is not working" from the first weeks. / **AVOID WHEN:** using early non-response as grounds to stop, or using early response as grounds to skip measurement.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р2 normative timelines, MODERATE for the review points, LOW for the recovery split.

### [PG26:RETENTION-STRUCTURE] Structure, not school, is the retention lever
**Move:** When dropout is the problem, change structure — time limit, manualized frame, explicit end — rather than switching schools, and treat a missed session inside the first four as the moment to act.
**APPLY WHEN:** attendance is slipping or a course is being designed for someone with a dropout history. / **AVOID WHEN:** presenting these percentages as prevalence for one clinic — the 19.7% mean is heterogeneous and the "half leave in the first seven sessions" figure is single-centre.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р3, MODERATE — 19.7% weighted mean; 17.8% time-limited vs 29.0% not; 18.3% manualized vs 28.3% not.

## E. Stuck course, therapist quality, closure

### [PG26:SWITCH-CHECK] Run the checklist and re-contract before any school switch
**Move:** Review at two to four weeks; at zero response by four to six weeks run the checklist — circumstances, co-occurring problems, attendance, formulation, adherence including practice — before any change of school, then re-contract inside the current method (name what is off, the desired outcome, why this technique, a concrete pace change, track a few sessions) and surface rupture markers (goal disagreement, task non-cooperation, visible strain) for in-session repair.
**APPLY WHEN:** no measurable shift after an honest trial; "therapy feels stuck". / **AVOID WHEN:** reacting to one hard week; or expecting rupture-repair training by itself to lift outcomes — it did not.
**Tier:** REQUIRES-THERAPIST / **Evidence:** Р2 + Р3 — normative timelines HIGH; rupture-repair r=.29 LOW.

### [PG26:RELAPSE-4S] Close a successful course with a relapse-prevention block
**Move:** End a course that worked with at least four additional sessions holding a staying-well plan, personal warning signs, and named unhelpful behaviours such as avoidance and rumination.
**APPLY WHEN:** closing a course that produced remission. / **AVOID WHEN:** there was response without remission — that carries higher relapse risk and needs more than a wrap-up block.
**Tier:** REQUIRES-THERAPIST / **Evidence:** Р3 normative guideline, HIGH as a norm, not as a measured effect.

### [PG26:STOP-RULES] Duration-plus-function stop thresholds and a planned buffer
**Move:** Schedule deep solo practice before rest days with a one-to-two-day recovery buffer, and stop and route to a session when thresholds trip — flooding lasting over 24 hours, functional decline, intrusive images, flashbacks, dissociation, panic, worsening sleep, suicidal thoughts; for writing, stop at over 45 minutes without resolution, closing tenser than opening, the same topic with no shift in perspective, or anxiety on skipping. If a mindfulness practice worsens things in someone with a trauma history, stop the practice rather than pushing through.
**APPLY WHEN:** any deep solo or writing practice; any report of post-practice distress. / **AVOID WHEN:** treating one-off intensity that resolves within a day, or early exhaustion, as a signal to switch method — temporary worsening is the baseline, not a fault.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р2 stop-markers LOW (community-derived); Р4 rumination mechanism LOW–MODERATE; the 24-hour flooding threshold is the safety protocol's own trigger.

### [PG26:FLAG-TIERS] Two-tier red flags; judge trajectory and reaction
**Move:** Hold a two-tier red-flag layer for both the live therapist and the AI layer itself — Tier 1 "leave now" (sexual or dual relationships, confidentiality breach, shaming, guaranteed results, working outside competence, impairment, harmful methods) and Tier 2 "give feedback first" (poor listening, no goals, no felt progress, months of unstructured talking for anxiety, ritual or trauma targets) — then judge the reaction: discusses it, stay; defends, leave.
**APPLY WHEN:** evaluating a therapist or the AI layer; judge content rather than tone, and trajectory rather than one session. / **AVOID WHEN:** using red-flag lists authored by therapy marketplaces (conflict of interest), or offering one-click "switch specialist" — that reproduces avoidance.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р4 — disciplinary data and negative-experience meta-analysis, MODERATE; the tier split itself is process consensus.

## F. Crisis and routing to a human

### [PG26:CRISIS-STOP] Crisis halts the frame on suspicion, without resumption
**Move:** On any risk suspicion — suicidality, self-harm, psychosis-like or manic states, violence, severe eating-disorder signals, a minor in distress — halt the role frame, give crisis contacts and the path to a human verbatim, and refuse to resume the normal mode even if the person walks it back.
**APPLY WHEN:** any hint, including indirect signals — generative response policy does not differentiate mid-range risk. / **AVOID WHEN:** a controlled therapeutic exercise with no risk signal — over-refusal breaks method fidelity and is its own failure.
**Tier:** REQUIRES-THERAPIST / **Evidence:** Р5 MODERATE for the policy; the walk-back failure mode (22 of 25 systems resumed) is 2023-era audit data, LOW.

### [PG26:ROUTE-HUMAN] Standing routing detectors, threshold, handoff format
**Move:** Route to a live human when PHQ-9 is above 9 (human in the loop mandatory), when someone asked for coping and ended up crying, when the bot missed and the person is more dysregulated, when the bot became the first emotional responder, on an "it sees me too well" intimacy surge, or on any crisis signal; hand off a FORMAT — theme, why it belongs in session, what to prepare, the question to ask, the debrief — and feed AI logs into therapy as session material.
**APPLY WHEN:** continuously, as standing detectors; mandatory for moderate-and-above severity. / **AVOID WHEN:** waiting for explicit crisis wording; selling the human as a symptom booster (guided vs unguided is MD −0.8 PHQ-9, below the minimal clinically important difference); or assuming a therapist's presence by itself removes risk.
**Tier:** REQUIRES-THERAPIST / **Evidence:** Р5 IPD network meta-analysis MODERATE for adherence (g≈0.26–0.29) and response at PHQ-9>9 (48% vs 37%); symptom-gain justification retired.

### [PG26:NO-DIAG-MED] Hard output bans: diagnosis, medication, mind-reading
**Move:** Hold four hard guardrails at all times — no diagnostic labels, no medication advice of any direction, no attribution of the person's emotions or mental states as fact, no treatment-plan changes.
**APPLY WHEN:** always. / **AVOID WHEN:** never — there is no exception, including "the user asked directly".
**Tier:** REQUIRES-THERAPIST / **Evidence:** Р5 — US statutory text (Illinois) bans all four, HIGH as law; the "guardrails held in one RCT" evidence is LOW.

### [PG26:VULNERABLE-OUT] Some populations sit outside the self-help tier entirely
**Move:** Place active suicidality, psychosis-like states, bipolar-pattern instability and severe eating-disorder presentations outside the AI/self-help tier altogether, and say so rather than adapting the format for them.
**APPLY WHEN:** any of those signals appear in the dossier or the conversation. / **AVOID WHEN:** using this as grounds to refuse psychoeducation to someone who is simply distressed — the exclusion is population-level, not distress-level.
**Tier:** REQUIRES-THERAPIST / **Evidence:** Р5 — exclusion criteria of the underlying trials plus regulatory text, MODERATE.

## G. Format positioning and AI-layer guardrails

### [PG26:AI-ANTIPAT] Block the documented AI iatrogenic patterns
**Move:** Run a layer above generation that blocks reassurance-feeding (with ritual-like patterns, reassurance is the compulsion — cap repeat queries on one worry), the rescuer role, infinite validation, endless sessions (explicit end plus a closing ritual, no engagement mechanics — the top harm mode is 2 a.m. spiralling), sycophancy (re-check beliefs across the whole context, reformulate in third person, give explicit permission to disagree), and silent lens switching (announce and justify every framework change).
**APPLY WHEN:** always, for any AI-mediated psychoeducation; long conversations especially. / **AVOID WHEN:** claiming a clinical-outcome benefit for these mitigations — none has been measured.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р5 — sycophancy roughly +49% affirmation versus humans, demoted to LOW (shared-method convergence, not replication); the mitigations themselves LOW.

### [PG26:POSITION-SELFHELP] Position as guided self-help, never as therapy
**Move:** State the frame explicitly — guided self-help and psychoeducation, not therapy, not a crisis service, not a source of clinical decisions — disclose non-clinician AI status, and keep a no-shame slot for "what did you discuss with the AI" in the live work.
**APPLY WHEN:** any framing, naming or onboarding text; mandatory for US-facing framing. / **AVOID WHEN:** using the word therapy, session of therapy, or "your therapist" about this format.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р4/Р5 statutory text and professional advisories, HIGH.

### [PG26:JURIS-BOUND] Do not transfer the regulatory layer outside US/UK
**Move:** Keep the regulatory reasoning bounded to the US (plus UK normative guidance) and say explicitly that EU and non-Western applicability is unassessed in this evidence base.
**APPLY WHEN:** any compliance, disclosure or "is this allowed" question. / **AVOID WHEN:** assuming a US self-help exemption transfers — Maine has no self-help exemption (research only), exemptions are confirmed for Illinois, Nevada and Rhode Island, Utah mandates disclosure, and at least seven states restrict as of 2026-08.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р5 law texts HIGH for the US facts; transfer beyond them is unmeasured, VERY LOW.

### [PG26:SAFETY-DESIGN] Safety architecture and adverse-event collection from day one
**Move:** Put risk assessment in a separate classifier or supervisor layer rather than the generation policy, keep crisis routing and method fidelity as separate axes so refusal is not the only safeguard, and collect adverse events from the first user through three loops (in-dialogue distress detection, periodic human check-ins, a spontaneous report channel), tracking reliable deterioration, escalations, stop reasons and emotional dependence; define success as help-seeking and referral uptake, not engagement time.
**APPLY WHEN:** designing or running any conversational psychoeducation deployment. / **AVOID WHEN:** treating an external classifier as validated (high-risk zero-shot sensitivity 0.05–0.06 — measure your own false-negative rate), or treating an absence of complaints as evidence of safety.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р5 — design consensus MODERATE, the evidence-collection payoff VERY LOW; adverse events were actively collected in 3 of 22 RCTs, and 42 of 51 completed trials went unreported.

## H. Number hygiene — this lens's own calibration acts

### [PG26:NUMBER-CHECK] Every cited number passes the corrections list first
**Move:** Before any lens cites a figure, check it against the verification corrections and use only the post-correction form, or drop the number and state the direction instead.
**APPLY WHEN:** any lens or verdict is about to quote an effect size, NNT, percentage or timeline. / **AVOID WHEN:** the number is being re-derived from the original source in the same breath — then the source, not this file, is authoritative.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р1–Р5 triple adversarial verification; this is a procedural rule, not an empirical claim.

### [PG26:ALLEGIANCE-DISCOUNT] Discount developer-affiliated and small-trial advantages
**Move:** Cut roughly 30% off effects from developer-affiliated trials, treat an advantage visible only in small trials as a small-study artifact, and downweight "several meta-analyses agree" where the author groups overlap.
**APPLY WHEN:** a method advantage is being claimed from a small, developer-run or author-overlapping literature. / **AVOID WHEN:** converting the discount into "the method does not work" — the correct output is a wider uncertainty band, not a negative claim.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р1/Р2 — allegiance r=.262, small-study asymmetry Egger p<0.001 in the depression literature, MODERATE.

### [PG26:EXPECT-BASE] State the base rates before anyone plans
**Move:** Put honest base rates on the table before a plan is built — response around 41% versus 17%, so most people do not respond inside about two months; 5–10% leave a course worse; adverse events in 5–20%, with unpleasant memories surfacing for about 58%; a couple of days of exhaustion after early deep work; clinicians catch only about 3% of deteriorations, which is why measurement exists.
**APPLY WHEN:** setting expectations, at the start and again at the first review. / **AVOID WHEN:** presenting the deterioration range as one number — published "therapy harm" figures span 5% to 50.9% purely by definition of harm.
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р1/Р2/Р3 — response and deterioration MODERATE (corrected NNT against deterioration 9.7 versus care-as-usual, 5.7 versus waitlist).

### [PG26:HONEST-CEILING] Name what this format cannot deliver
**Move:** Say plainly that unguided formats are not better than usual care, that app engagement collapses (69.4% at day one to 3.3% at day 30, with trial engagement roughly four times real-world), and that chatbot effects versus inactive controls peak around week 8 and are gone at three months.
**APPLY WHEN:** anyone proposes replacing live work with this format, or expects durable symptom change from it. / **AVOID WHEN:** turning this into "AI support does nothing" — versus inactive controls the short-term effect is real (g≈0.26–0.31, NNT 11–12), and versus active controls the finding is "effect not shown", not "no effect".
**Tier:** SELF / **Evidence:** Р4/Р5 — retention HIGH, chatbot-versus-inactive MODERATE and publication-bias-clean, versus-active LOW.
