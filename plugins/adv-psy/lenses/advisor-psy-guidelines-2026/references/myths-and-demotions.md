# advisor-psy-guidelines-2026 — myths and demotions

What triple adversarial verification (Codex GPT-5.6 Sol + Fable 5 + Grok 4.5) removed, demoted or bounded across reports Р1–Р5. No lens may reuse a pre-correction version of anything below. Each entry states what to say instead.

## Refuted — never cite again in any form

- **"Solo parts work is fine as long as protectors agree, and people crash at 2–3 weeks."** Anecdote-derived pseudo-precision. The 2–3-week timeline does not exist. Instead: operation-level tier boundary plus the state gate [PG26:STATE-GATE] (Р2).
- **"Online skills groups raised suicide-attempt risk, HR 1.29."** Outcome substitution — the hazard ratio describes a different outcome. Never reuse the number. Instead: solo skills evidence is weaker; harm was not shown (Р2).
- **Homework-completion benchmarks: "41% complete", "51% vs 72% in a 2026 survey".** The 41% traces to a 1998 non-cognitive study; the 2026 survey figures do not exist. Instead: no completion benchmark exists — cite setup quality, congruence and micro-format [PG26:MICRO-TASK] (Р4).
- **"Behavioural assignments are completed more than written ones."** One small study plus an analogue sample. Instead: no strong format-completability data (Р4).
- **"Guided self-help is the main lever, OR 2.76."** The odds ratio was lifted from a different 2014 paper; the correct estimate is roughly half that and small (Р4).
- **Leichsenring 2003 d=1.46 as support for schema-focused work.** It is a psychodynamic pre-post figure. Instead: Bamelis 2014 and Zhang 2023, always with magnitudes (Р1).
- **Grief-approach superiority for any brand.** Retracted: Hao 2024 NMA shows five significant modalities with overlapping CIs. Instead: works at elevated prolonged-grief symptoms; no winning brand [PG26:GRIEF-GATE] (Р1).
- **"Social skills training for loneliness does not work."** k=2 — unmeasured, not refuted; the anchor moved from Masi 2011 to Hickin 2021 (Р1).
- **The alliance d=0.57 versus method d≤0.20 contrast.** Correlational and randomized numbers are not commensurable. Instead: alliance as association only [PG26:JUDGE-PROCESS] (Р1).
- **RR 0.39 / NNT 20 for the protective effect against deterioration.** Publication-bias-uncorrected and roughly twice overstated; 89% of the response data were imputed. Instead: NNT 9.7 (care as usual) / 5.7 (waitlist) [PG26:EXPECT-BASE] (Р3).
- **"Dropout 15% vs 42.6% proves human-in-the-loop."** One German RCT. Instead: argue from meta-analytic adherence g≈0.26–0.29 [PG26:ROUTE-HUMAN] (Р5).

## Demoted — direction survives, weight does not

- **Alliance r=.278**: the upgrade to MODERATE was invalid → LOW. The publication-bias check behind it used a funnel plot plus fail-safe N, with only 2% negative effects showing — a selectivity signal, not a clean corpus. Say "not tested with a valid method" (Р3).
- **Homework presence d=0.48**: MODERATE → LOW (Р3).
- **Deterioration base rate and the protective effect**: → LOW (Р3).
- **Removal-dismantling d≈0.01**: MODERATE → LOW; the chain was circular (a narrative figure plus a shared-author source). Instead: "parts as good as the whole" (Bell 2013) (Р1).
- **Compassion-focused work for self-criticism (g=0.51)**: MODERATE → LOW, passive-control-driven. Instead: better than nothing, superiority over active treatment not shown (Р1).
- **Intolerance-of-uncertainty experiments**: → LOW (N=60, waitlist, developer-authored); transdiagnostic version VERY LOW (N=37, completer-only) (Р1).
- **Crisis-failure and sycophancy in generative systems**: MODERATE → LOW. "Replicated across 5/8/17/29 systems" was wrappers over two-to-four base models sharing one method; the dose-response upgrade came from a VERY LOW preprint (Р5).
- **The sycophancy → ritual-undermining chain**: numbers from an outdated preprint; the clinical link is extrapolation. Keep it as a qualitative risk category only [PG26:AI-ANTIPAT] (Р4).
- **"Zero effect versus active control"** → LOW, and must be phrased "effect not shown, not disproven" (n=8, no equivalence margin) (Р5).

## Bounded — true only inside stated limits

- **Interchangeability of methods** holds for depression only, and not for acceptance-based or schema lenses, which were absent from the network (Р1). [PG26:TARGET-NOT-BRAND]
- **Zhang 2023 schema meta-analysis**: "group works, individual is zero" over-reads it — both arms are non-significant, the pooled g=0.359 barely clears zero, and the corpus is not a pure personality-pattern sample (Р1).
- **BOOTS**: p=0.27 and d=0.15 are not formal equivalence; the design was superiority, the cohort motivated (Р2).
- **Parts-model trials**: no advantage shown, small base — the 2026 trial won on engagement only (Р2).
- **Twice-weekly sessions**: acute speed and retention only; the advantage is gone at 12–24 months and it is not cost-effective. Dose and density are not in conflict: total count is unrelated to outcome, while acute density plus between-session consolidation works (Р3). [PG26:DOSE-DENSITY]
- **Off-track ROM alerts**: the detector literature is one line, not two (Barkham 2023 reproduces de Jong 2021); with an independent scale the effect goes to zero, and part of it is regression to the mean. But "no independent ROM replications" was also wrong — six independent meta-analyses give 0.13–0.27. Circularity inflates the subgroup claim, not the mean (Р3). [PG26:TRIAD-ROM]
- **"Therapy harm" prevalence** spans 5% to 50.9% purely by definition. Judge a multi-point trajectory, never one session (Р4). [PG26:EXPECT-BASE]
- **α=1.00 for an external risk classifier** is rerun reproducibility at temperature 0, not accuracy. Expert agreement tops out around α 0.78; high-risk zero-shot sensitivity is 0.05–0.06 (Р5). [PG26:SAFETY-DESIGN]
- **Delusion-confirmation score 0.91** sits on a 0–2 scale, just below "supports" (range 0.26–1.34). Direction stands; the magnitude was roughly doubled (Р5).
- **"0 of 29 agents passed"** is a rubric artifact — 51.72% met the relaxed criteria; always attach the caveat. And the same audit measured response POLICY, not risk recognition: do not cite it as "models cannot recognize mid-range risk" (Р5).
- **Maine has no self-help exemption** (research only). Exemptions confirmed for Illinois, Nevada, Rhode Island; at least seven states by 2026-08, including Tennessee SB 1580 with a private right of action (Р5). [PG26:JURIS-BOUND]
- **NNT 2.7/3.1 from the Jordan trial** is not a benchmark — low-and-middle-income setting, near-inactive comparator, scripted bot plus five human calls. Never place it next to NNT 11–12. The Zenny trial (N=1345) is excluded from depression rows: it measured well-being, never depression (Р5).
- **Single-study and single-centre figures** to mark as such: per-modality burden percentages, "half of dropouts leave in the first seven sessions", "90% of clients replay recordings", the 99-versus-28 marketplace poll (a Mentimeter show of hands), the professional-body 77% (psychologist report at 6.3% completion, not patient prevalence) (Р2/Р4).
- **Geography and funding**: the corpus is entirely Western, and country is a significant moderator. "Zero industry-funded" does not mean no conflict of interest — app patents, vendor ties and monitoring-lobby funding are present, and two completed monitoring/rupture RCTs were never published (Р3).

## Disputed — do not present as settled

- **"Common factors ≥ modality, with a fixed exception list."** The exception list is not settled. Instead: lenses as a repertoire of questions, routing as hypothesis [PG26:ROUTING-AS-HYPOTHESES] (Р2).
- **"Experiential techniques are the core, and 'lite' delivery is a failure mode."** The lighter protocols are recognized manualized variants (Р2).
- **Generative-subgroup unanimity in the chatbot safety literature** is false — sources diverge, and part of the corpus is pre-LLM-era with n=5. Present it as divergence (Р5).

## Anti-recommendations — framings to keep out of the council

- **Therapy-marketplace red-flag lists.** Conflict of interest by construction; a one-click "switch specialist" button reproduces avoidance [PG26:FLAG-TIERS] (Р4).
- **Polemic recomputations of school-versus-school effect sizes.** Use gates and operations, not duelling statistics (Р2).
- **Community testimonials as prevalence.** Forums yield failure modes; the loud cases are phase-fit artifacts (Р2).
- **Stage models of grief and any positive reframe of grief.** The entry condition is measurement, and in uncomplicated grief the intervention effect is near zero and can do harm [PG26:GRIEF-GATE] (Р1/Р2).
- **Vendor self-report reaching us through journalism** (for example a "~0.15% weekly" figure for conversations with suicidal planning). Scale illustration only, never a rate (Р5).
- **"No proven unacceptable harm."** Wrong framing. Say: the safety of this format is not established — adverse events were actively collected in 3 of 22 RCTs, and 42 of 51 completed trials went unreported. Safeguards substitute for missing data (Р5). [PG26:SAFETY-DESIGN]

## Documented failure modes and iatrogenic patterns

- **Shaming a missed practice.** Non-completion is a design failure of the task, not a character finding [PG26:MICRO-TASK] (Р4).
- **Opening a session with new material before reviewing the last practice.** Teaches that practice does not matter [PG26:SKELETON] (Р4).
- **Solo escalation of an exposure hierarchy.** A failed exposure reinforces the fear [PG26:ERP-NO-ESC] (Р2).
- **Prompted retelling of fresh trauma.** Harm signal around OR 2.5 [PG26:NO-FRESH-DEBRIEF] (Р4).
- **Pushing through worsening in mindfulness practice with a trauma history.** Stop the practice, not "try harder" [PG26:STOP-RULES] (Р4).
- **Literalizing parts language** where reality testing or identity stability is in question (Р2). [PG26:STATE-GATE]
- **Reassurance loops.** With ritual-like patterns, giving reassurance is feeding the compulsion [PG26:AI-ANTIPAT] (Р5).
- **Endless availability without a closing ritual.** The top-reported harm mode is opening the app at 2 a.m., spiralling, and closing it mid-collapse (Р4/Р5).
- **Silent lens switching.** Changing framework without announcing it disorients on its own (Р2).
- **Resuming normal mode after a risk walk-back.** In the 2023 audit, 22 of 25 systems did exactly this [PG26:CRISIS-STOP] (Р5).
- **Treating an absence of complaints as evidence of safety** — the corpus under-reports harm by construction, with 82% of safety-relevant registrations unreported (Р5).
