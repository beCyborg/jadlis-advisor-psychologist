# advisor-attachment — myths, demotions, and documented failure modes

What this tradition itself refutes, what external verification (PG26) took away from it, and the ways it is known to go wrong. Read this file before letting the lens speak confidently about grief, typology, or its own reach.

## 1. Myths this tradition refutes from the inside

**Reading personality off a depressed snapshot.** The classic error the manual is built against: passivity, irritability, avoidance and self-described defectiveness during an episode are largely the state. Personality measures did not predict short-term outcome (Zuckerman 1980), personality-disordered patients improved comparably (Markowitz 1998), and apparent traits receded over maintenance (Cyranowski 2004). Any verdict on "what I am really like" taken while low is data about the low state. → [ATT:STATE-NOT-TRAIT]

**Working on several problems at once.** Choosing two or more focal areas dilutes the effort into none. This is a stance, not a trial result — but the failure mode it names (nothing moves, everything is touched) is the manual's most repeated warning. → [ATT:FORMULATE]

**"I have tried everything."** Almost never accurate on inspection: attempts were half-hearted, or options were never generated because discouragement narrowed the list. → [ATT:DECISION-ANALYSIS]

**Expecting to be read without speaking.** Mind-reading assumptions destabilize relationships, and unexpressed annoyance guarantees the behaviour continues. Silence and sulking are not neutral — they are messages with worse resolution. → [ATT:COMM-ANALYSIS], [ATT:ASSERT-VALID]

**Venting as a template.** What is safe to say in a protected review is not a script for the relationship. Repeated angry or sad outbursts reinforce the cycle they discharge. → [ATT:AFFECT-ELICIT], [ATT:ANGER-GRADED]

**Fear of being overwhelmed by grief.** Near-universal and near-always wrong: expressed feeling loses force rather than gaining it. Two adjacent myths go with it — that working on a loss disrespects the dead person, and that the goal is to end mourning. It is not; mourning continues for years, and what is targeted is the blockage, not the grief. → [ATT:GRIEF-RECON]

**"Biological versus psychological."** A false split — psychotherapy measurably changes brain function (Brody 2001, Martin 2001). Also filed here: alcohol as self-treatment for low mood, which depresses mood further and raises suicide risk.

**Ageism and situational dismissal.** "The old cannot change" is contradicted by trial evidence; "who would not be depressed with cancer" collapses on the fact that most seriously ill people are not depressed; "pregnancy means unconditional well-being" is false — roughly 10% of pregnant women meet criteria for major depression, and it does not spontaneously remit.

**"It is just supportive listening."** Half true and worth conceding: the method shares common factors, but adds systematic interpersonal strategies applied consistently. Technique overlap is not equivalence — and note that hundreds of psychotherapies were never efficacy-tested at all.

## 2. Demotions and corrections applied from outside

**The star ratings are 2007 vintage.** The manual's own scale (4★ down to 0★) predates the updated ratings in the 2018 edition and everything trialed since. Treat every star in this lens as a floor with unknown drift, not a current figure.

**The fixed session count.** The manual presents an exact number as itself therapeutic. It was never tested against open-ended delivery, and PG26 rejects planning a fixed count outright — steer by trajectory. What survives is structure per se (17.8% dropout in time-limited formats against 29.0%), not the number.

**"Interchangeable methods" does not travel.** Between-method equivalence for depression (Barth 2013, d = 0.01…−0.30) is MODERATE for depression ONLY. It does not extend to trauma-memory or ritual-shaped targets, nor to acceptance- and schema-based methods absent from that network. This lens cannot claim parity outside depressive-interpersonal territory.

**Grief-brand superiority is retracted.** Hao 2024 (55 studies, N=10,330) shows five modalities with overlapping intervals; the earlier claim that one school leads for grief is demoted to LOW. The operational rule is entry-condition, not brand: measure prolonged-grief symptoms first, work only above the threshold, and route the exposure component to a clinician. For uncomplicated grief the intervention effect is near zero and potentially harmful. → [ATT:GRIEF-SCREEN], [ATT:GRIEF-RECON]

**Monitoring is weaker than it looks.** Routine outcome measurement sits at d≈0.13–0.27; the off-track-alert payoff falls to non-significance with an independent scale, and the deterioration-prevention claim is VERY LOW (de Jong 2021 fulltext: OR 1.16, p=0.07). [ATT:SCALE-TRACK] runs because it is cheap and aimed at the tail, not because it prevents anything.

**Alliance and homework figures.** Alliance r=.278 (about 8% of variance) is LOW and correlational — never usable as proof that method choice is irrelevant. Homework presence d=0.48 is LOW, and there is NO reliable completion benchmark; the once-cited compliance percentages were traced to the wrong sources. This lens's refusal of formal homework is compatible with the evidence, which favours quality over quantity (g=1.07 against 0.51) and treats non-completion as a design failure.

**Comparative dysthymia claims are dose-unbalanced.** Browne 2002 pitted 12 sessions against 2+ years of medication. The cost-effectiveness conclusion may stand; the head-to-head reading does not.

**Cross-cultural universality is thin.** One or two trials per culture, with the technique adaptations authored by local trainees rather than trialed separately. The Uganda trial dropped the isolation category entirely as culturally meaningless — evidence that a focal area can be a local artifact.

## 3. Anti-recommendations — framings to keep out of this lens

**Popular attachment typology.** Do not import anxious / avoidant / secure as personality types. The canonical manual for this lens never measures attachment at all; the typology arrives from popular sources, and in research attachment is measured dimensionally and relative to a specific relationship, not as a fixed trait of a person. A lens that starts typing people has abandoned both its own manual and the measurement literature. This is the single most likely contamination of this lens.

**Childhood-origin excavation, transference readings, dream work.** Explicitly outside the method. Where the working relationship is used at all ([ATT:DYAD-FEEDBACK]) it is a live behaviour sample, never an interpretation of the past — and that operation is therapist-side anyway.

**Calling isolation "interpersonal deficits" to the person.** The manual's own label insults, and the manual says so. Use isolation or loneliness, and prefer recasting the case as a transition where that fits.

**Presenting this method for targets where it scored zero.** Substance problems (two negative RCTs), restriction-type eating patterns, and chronic low mood as monotherapy. The authors state the first plainly and warn against marketing it there.

**Positive reframes anywhere near a death.** The balanced-audit step of transition work ([ATT:TRANS-WORK]) exists to correct a memory distortion about a ROLE. Pointed at a bereavement it becomes silver-lining talk, which is prohibited in this council regardless of tradition.

**Claims of certification.** No official credential for this method exists, so any "certified" claim exceeds what the field offers. Relatedly, lectures and workshops alone do not change practitioner behaviour (Davis 1999) — supervised recorded cases are the active ingredient.

## 4. Documented failure modes

- **Non-response converted into self-blame.** The known pattern is that people who do not respond conclude they are the failure and abandon all treatment. The counter-move exists precisely for this. → [ATT:NONRESPONSE-REFRAME]
- **Practice reframed as homework.** Non-completion makes people feel like bad patients and predicts dropout; the manual keeps out-of-session work an invitation for this reason. → [ATT:DEFICIT-REBUILD]
- **Reopening an impasse and expecting relief.** Open disagreement typically increases first. Someone unprepared for that reads the increase as proof the method failed. → [ATT:DISPUTE-STAGE]
- **Confrontation into an unsafe relationship.** Naming a transgression to an abusive or currently dangerous partner escalates. Safety planning precedes, and it is not a solo operation. → [ATT:TRANSGRESSION]
- **Rehearsal replacing the attempt.** Role-play loops feel productive and substitute for the encounter; a polished script also breeds the illusion of controlling the other person's response. → [ATT:ROLEPLAY]
- **Pathologizing ordinary mourning.** Sadness, broken sleep and reduced functioning that resolve over months are adaptive; discouraging them is iatrogenic. Complication markers are a severe phase past two months, or not functioning at work or with family two to three months on. → [ATT:GRIEF-SCREEN]
- **Illness psychoeducation aimed at someone who already over-identifies as ill.** More illness framing is the wrong direction there; the redirect is to the interpersonal trigger. → [ATT:BINGE-CONTEXT], [ATT:SICKROLE]
- **Rhythm work sold as a substitute for medication**, or started after an activated episode is already running — it prevents, it does not treat. → [ATT:SRM]
- **Response mistaken for remission.** A large drop that lands short of the remission band is still near relapse threshold, and continuation planning is a clinician decision. → [ATT:WHY-BETTER], [ATT:MAINT-CONTRACT]
- **The format's own iatrogenic risk.** Adverse events in psychotherapy run 5–20%, 5–10% leave worse, and practitioners catch roughly 3 of 40 deteriorations. Safety data for a text-interface format do not exist at all — the honest statement is that safety of this format is not established, not that harm was not observed.
