# advisor-cbt — core principles, ranked by evidence

The lens's mechanism claim: what a situation is construed to MEAN drives the emotion, the body state and the next behaviour; construal runs at three depths — the fast appraisal stream, the cross-situational if-then rules, and the absolute self/other/world statements underneath — and durable change needs both the surface stream and the behaviour that keeps the rules unfalsified. Onset is a latent rule meeting a matching stressor; maintenance is behavioural as much as cognitive, because avoidance blocks precisely the experiences that would contradict the rule and biased processing discounts the rest automatically, as a symptom rather than a choice.

That claim is not evenly evidenced. Below, principles are sorted by what the evidence actually supports, using the corrected figures from the 2026 guidelines layer — several numbers this tradition still repeats were demoted or refuted there and appear here only in post-correction form. Two indirectness layers cap EVERY row: the trials ran on clinical populations delivered by live clinicians, and no study has tested a clinician-free format. Procedures transfer; effect sizes do not.

## Tier 1 — MODERATE: the load-bearing parts

### Doing this rather than nothing changes depressed mood — d ≈ −0.62 to −0.92 vs waitlist; response 41% vs 17%
Bona fide psychotherapy for depressed mood beats waiting and usual care at MODERATE certainty. The honest reading of the response figures is the one the tradition skips: most people do NOT respond inside about two months. Everything the lens offers is built on top of that base rate — [CBT:MOOD-TRACK], [CBT:VALUES-GOALS].

### Behavioural activation as a standalone component — MODERATE
Scheduling and doing value-linked activity, independent of any thought work, holds as its own component. This is the single best-supported move in the lens and the reason the workbook sequences activity BEFORE cognitive work in low mood — [CBT:ACT-SCHED].

### Cognitive restructuring as a standalone component — MODERATE
Testing appraisals against evidence also holds as its own component. What does NOT follow is that the specific worksheet, column count or question order matters; the component is evidenced, the format is not — [CBT:TR7], [CBT:SOCRATIC-EVAL], [CBT:BEH-EXP].

### Graded exposure for fear and avoidance — MODERATE, and here schools are not interchangeable
Graduated, repeated, prolonged approach with safety behaviours dropped is the best-supported intervention class for fear. It is also the place where the "all bona fide methods are equal" finding fails: ritual-shaped and trauma-memory targets need protocol-specific treatment. Design and escalation sit with the therapist; executing and logging an agreed hierarchy does not — [CBT:EXPOSURE], [CBT:PANIC-INDUCE], [CBT:SAFETY-ID], [CBT:FEAR-LADDER].

### No superiority over other bona fide methods for depressed mood — between-method d = 0.01 … −0.30
The equivalence finding (198 trials, N≈15,000) means the lens should be chosen for target fit and personal acceptability, never because it is "the evidence-based one". The equivalence itself is bounded: depressed mood only, and it does not extend to trauma-related or ritual-shaped targets, nor to schools absent from the network.

### Protocol fidelity is not the active ingredient — adherence→outcome null
The best-evidenced negative finding in the surrounding literature: how faithfully a protocol is followed does not predict outcome. Judge by what changed in the episode and in the behaviour, not by how complete the worksheet was — [CBT:NOSHIFT], [CBT:ACTION-PLAN].

## Tier 2 — LOW: real signals, smaller than the tradition assumes

### Between-session practice — d = 0.48, demoted to LOW; quality g = 1.07 vs quantity g = 0.51
Homework presence still points the right way but no longer carries the weight this method puts on it, and there is NO valid completion benchmark — the percentages that circulate in CBT training material were traced to a non-CBT 1998 study and to a survey that does not exist. What survives: setup quality, one step, explicit rationale, obstacle plan, review of quality rather than of compliance — [CBT:ACTION-PLAN].

### Measurement with feedback — d ≈ 0.13–0.27 overall, off-track subgroup contested toward zero
Routine tracking is worth doing because it is cheap and aimed at the tail, not because it drives outcome; the deterioration-prevention claim specifically did not survive (feedback→deterioration OR 1.16, p = 0.07). Any alert must terminate in a pre-written action ending with a human — [CBT:MOOD-TRACK], [CBT:RELAPSE-PLAN].

### The working relationship — r = .278, about 8% of variance, LOW
Alliance is an association, not a proven lever, and the correlational figure cannot be compared with method effect sizes from trials. Repair work is hygiene: unaddressed negative reactions predict disengagement, and that is enough reason to ask — [CBT:FEEDBACK-REPAIR].

### Socratic questioning and guided discovery
Linked to symptom change within treatment and preferred by clients — correlational, inside packages, never isolated. The prohibition on arguing rests on a mechanism argument, not on a trial: the degree of distortion is unknowable in advance and disputing invalidates — [CBT:SOCRATIC-EVAL].

### Experience beats discussion; imagery beats words
Experiential disconfirmation outperforms verbal evaluation, and imagery moves emotion more strongly than verbal processing. Both are method-level findings that justify the ordering of moves, not the specific scripts — [CBT:BEH-EXP], [CBT:IMAGERY-KIT].

### Relapse-prevention work and continued skill use
Relapse-focused sessions delay recurrence and continued use of the skills protects under later stressful events. Both come from within-tradition follow-up studies, and the norm of closing a successful course with a dedicated staying-well block is normative guidance rather than a measured effect — [CBT:RELAPSE-PLAN], [CBT:SELF-THERAPY].

## Tier 3 — method canon only: the technique exists, the trial does not

Everything here is standard practice inside protocols that were tested WHOLE. None was separately tested; a "failed" instance is data about the formulation, not evidence against the method.

- The organising maps: three-level cognition, five-part model, the conceptualisation diagram, the helpless/unlovable/worthless grouping — frames for generating hypotheses, not verified entities — [CBT:5PART], [CBT:BELIEF-MAP].
- The written apparatus: thought records and worksheets in either format, notes and coping cards, credit lists — [CBT:TR7], [CBT:THERAPY-NOTES].
- The reattribution set: illness framing, the responsibility pie, fair-comparison correction, the cognitive continuum — [CBT:REATTRIB], [CBT:RESP-PIE], [CBT:CB-STRENGTHEN].
- The twelve-distortion list: a teaching device, explicitly optional; labels overlap and one thought carries several — [CBT:TR7].
- Skill-versus-cognition testing, advantage/disadvantage grids, problem-solving routing, the anxiety stance and the arousal-management doses (the four-minute breathing figure is a physiological rationale, not an outcome) — [CBT:SKILL-VS-COG], [CBT:ADV-DISADV], [CBT:PROBLEM-SOLVE], [CBT:AWARE], [CBT:REGULATE].
- The mood inventories used to run all of it: author-constructed, face-valid, never validated as diagnostic instruments — and the whole progress logic rides on them, scored by the same mind whose processing is the target — [CBT:MOOD-TRACK].
- The therapist-tier deep techniques: downward arrow, historical review, point-counterpoint, deliberate affect intensification, early-memory restructuring — canon and clinical experience, with no trial evidence cited for the specific procedures — [CBT:CORE-BELIEF-MOD], [CBT:AFFECT-HEIGHTEN], [CBT:MEM-RESTRUCT].

## Tier 4 — extrapolation: state it as such whenever cited

- **The positive track.** Building realistic adaptive beliefs alongside weakening negative ones is presented as co-equal with the traditional track, but its evidence is early-stage; broaden-and-build research is imported from another literature — [CBT:POSITIVE-DATA].
- **Gratitude and planned kindness.** The weakest-sourced material in either book: cited generically, without references, one unnamed study — [CBT:POSITIVE-DATA].
- **The companion-belief protocol.** Growing a new belief instead of disputing the old is clinical consensus inside an evidence-based frame; the year-long case trajectory is an illustration, not an outcome measure — [CBT:CB-STRENGTHEN].
- **Guilt and shame components, disclosure, the forgiveness letter.** Authors' protocols with no trials cited; two of them are tiered to the therapist because of what they open, not because of what they prove — [CBT:GUILT-SHAME-PATH], [CBT:BREAK-SILENCE], [CBT:FORGIVE-LETTER].
- **The automaticity promise.** That roughly 20–40 written records produce spontaneous balanced thinking is a clinical observation, never measured.
- **Dose figures.** Six to sixteen sessions for uncomplicated presentations is a planning heuristic; routine practice runs 4–26 with a naturalistic mean under 5, and no fixed count should be planned — steer by trajectory.

## Sequencing — the one ordering claim the lens will defend

The order is better supported than any individual technique, because it follows the tiers above: put the MODERATE components first and the canon-only ones later.

1. Measurement starts at baseline and never stops — everything after this is judged against it, on multi-week trends only — [CBT:MOOD-TRACK].
2. In low mood, activity before analysis: scheduling and doing come first, cognitive work once things are moving — [CBT:ACT-SCHED].
3. Naming and regulating before depth: label the mood, catch the thought, learn to stay in the situation — [CBT:MOOD-ID], [CBT:AT-CATCH], [CBT:REGULATE].
4. Surface before rules before beliefs: the top level changes faster and usually lifts mood, and belief-level work is explicitly optional in the workbook and gated twice in the manual — [CBT:TR7], [CBT:BEH-EXP], [CBT:BELIEF-MAP].
5. When verbal work produces head-level agreement only, stop talking and run a test — that is the point at which the evidence favours experience over discussion — [CBT:BEH-EXP].
6. Anything that reactivates traumatic material never enters this sequence; it leaves it, toward the live session.

## Boundary conditions — where this lens's own claims stop

- **Grief.** No superiority for this method survives here; grief work belongs after measurement of prolonged-grief symptoms and, when confirmed, is therapist-led. In ordinary grief, protocol work has near-zero effect and can do harm. The lens contributes measurement and a route out, nothing else.
- **Trauma material.** Fenced, not treated. Both books route it away and neither supplies a processing protocol; rescripting is therapist-performed even inside therapy — [CBT:MEM-RESTRUCT], [CBT:EXPOSURE].
- **Unguided use.** Self-help without guidance is weaker than every guided format and not better than usual care, though large-scale data do not show it as harmful in aggregate (deterioration 5.8% vs 9.1%). Negative effects are reported in a wide band across self-help interventions and roughly one in five self-help books carries advice judged harmful — so the format, not the school, is the risk. Above a PHQ-9 of 9 a human in the loop is mandatory, for adherence and deterioration monitoring, not for extra symptom gain (the guided-versus-unguided symptom difference is under the minimal clinically important threshold).
- **Population ceiling.** The canonical manual explicitly excludes suicidality, self-harm, substance use, youth and older adults and says other presentations need protocol variations it does not supply; the workbook adds that poor concentration and memory make it unusable and routes to care. Do not stretch these moves sideways.
- **Delivery assumptions.** Everything here presumes literacy, tolerance for writing, and weeks-to-months of adherence — and a second person managing dosing, affect and repair. The SELF tiering in this lens is an adaptation of therapist-delivered technique, and both books carry the same disclosure: neither has a trial of itself.
- **Cultural loading.** The method encodes rationality, individual agency and the scientific method as defaults, and its evidence corpus is almost entirely Western. Context — poverty, an abusive environment, systemic constraint — is named as an interfering variable rather than a target, and interpersonal outcomes are the least assured thing it produces.
