# advisor-common-factors — myths, demotions and documented failure modes

What this tradition has retracted about itself, plus the numbers the guidelines lens (PG26)
corrected downward. Nothing here may be restated in a stronger form elsewhere in the lens.

## 1. Retracted by the authors themselves

- **"Resistance" and "denial" as client traits.** Never confirmed by research. The authors publicly
  retract the label they used in their own first two editions and split it into sustain talk (about
  the topic) and discord (about you) — both jointly produced, both turnable up and down by what the
  helper does [CF:DISCORD-SCAN]. Say "this exchange produced more sustain talk", never "he is resistant".
- **Confrontational "breaking down defences."** It exacerbates exactly what it targets and would be
  treated as malpractice for most other presenting problems. The associated model of substance
  problems as immature defences is rejected outright.
- **The decisional-balance exercise as a motivator.** Listing all pros and cons has neither a
  theoretical nor an empirical basis as a change aid and *decreases* commitment in ambivalent people.
  It survives only as the instrument of deliberate neutrality — never as a warm-up, and never as a
  "test" of whether someone is ready, because measuring ambivalence that way is not inert.
- **"Hit bottom" / "wait until they have suffered enough" / "families are powerless until the person
  is ready."** Guilt and shame rarely fuel change; fear and shame shut people down. Anything built on
  making someone feel bad enough to change is out.
- **Motivation as a trait or a fuel gauge.** It is generated inside a relationship and is specific to
  one concrete action — "motivated for treatment" is too coarse to mean anything [CF:SEEDFRAME].
- **"No commitment language, no change."** All seven change-talk types favour subsequent change, and a
  favourable balance predicts action without any declared intention. Pressing for "I will" can undo
  motivational progress [CF:COMMIT-LISTEN].
- **"Feedback doesn't work."** A misreading of the check-up, which failed at its designed purpose
  (routing people into treatment) while substantially reducing drinking outside treatment.

## 2. Misidentifications of the method itself

- **"It is just good listening."** The commonest misunderstanding, and training that under-teaches the
  directional component measurably reduces efficacy. Reflection without direction is the hollowed-out
  version [CF:DIRREF].
- **"It is nondirective / it is client-centred counselling / it is the transtheoretical stages of
  change / it is assessment feedback / it is a quick technique."** All four are rejected by name. It
  is directional but not directive; the stages-of-change model belongs to a different tradition.
- **"It is the opposite of giving advice."** Unskillful direction is the problem, not advice — trained
  nurses wrongly concluded that advising a new mother about feeding an infant would violate the method.
  The rule is the permission gate [CF:ASK-PERMISSION], not silence.
- **"OARS are neutral tools."** All four carry direction whether or not you notice. Neutrality has to
  be chosen deliberately and is not achieved by "just not giving advice"; inadvertent nudging is the
  documented failure mode precisely because it is invisible to both parties.
- **"I attended the workshop, so I do this."** Workshops alone barely move coded practice; self-report
  of skill errs upward; certification proves competence at one moment, not current fidelity, and drift
  is the default. Personality, professional background and degrees do not predict who learns it, and
  clinical experience can *hurt* coding accuracy because clinicians infer meaning instead of recording
  what was said [CF:PRACTICE-LOOP].

## 3. Numbers demoted or corrected (PG26 verification)

Use only the post-correction form.

- **Alliance r = .278 → LOW.** An earlier upgrade was invalid. About 8% of variance. The contrast
  "alliance d = 0.57 versus method d ≤ 0.20" is **retracted** — correlational and randomized numbers
  are not commensurable. Alliance is an association and a validator, never a lever you can pull.
- **"Common factors ≥ modality, with an exception list" → DISPUTED.** Do not present that list as
  settled. This lens is a repertoire of questions; routing arrows are hypotheses, not assignments.
- **Homework presence d = 0.48 → LOW**, and there is **no reliable completion benchmark**. The "41%
  completion" figure traces to a 1998 non-CBT study, and the "51% vs 72%, 2026 survey" figure does not
  exist. Judge a between-contact task on setup quality and on what changed, not on compliance [CF:BAP].
- **Between-method equivalence is a depression-only finding.** It does not extend to fear- or
  ritual-driven targets, nor to schools absent from the source network.
- **Developer allegiance inflates effects by roughly 30%.** This tradition, whose trial base is largely
  run by its own community, is not exempt; "many reviews agree" is downweighted where authorship overlaps.
- **Delivery-mode indirectness.** No study tested a clinician-free format. Procedures transfer to solo
  use; effect sizes do not. Every solo application here operates outside the evidence base.
- **Grief.** Superiority of any single brand for grief is retracted (five modalities with overlapping
  intervals). Grief work has an entry condition — measured elevated prolonged-grief symptoms — and no
  solo format exists. For ordinary grief, protocols are near-zero in effect and potentially harmful.

## 4. Anti-recommendations — what not to read, cite or run

- **The circulating Russian translation is the 3rd edition.** The persistence/growth thesis, the
  "empowerment" and "fixing reflex" vocabulary, and the renaming of the pendulum move are
  4th-edition-only and cannot be cited from it. There are **no Russian translations of the
  4th-edition material** — any Russian-language practice text or between-session task has to be
  written from scratch, not translated on the fly.
- **Page numbers.** Edition-specific, and the e-book pagination does not match print. Cite chapters,
  boxes and figures, not pages.
- **Certification and "MI-informed" branding as evidence of quality.** The method was deliberately
  never trademarked; there is no authority checking anyone. A claim of doing it well carries no
  information without observed, coded practice.
- **Decisional-balance worksheets, pros-and-cons apps, "stages of change" quizzes.** See §1 and §2 —
  they belong to other traditions or to the retracted layer.
- **Any framing that scores a person's readiness and then assigns them a method.** Algorithmic
  matching has no prospective validation.

## 5. Documented failure modes and iatrogenic patterns

- **Coercive institutional use.** Mandatory "motivational interviews" run in Danish departure centres
  to push denied asylum seekers to leave: few signed the voluntary forms, those who did rarely left,
  and both sides found the sessions aversive. Without a goal consistent with the person's own values
  the technique is inert. Relabelling an institutional goal as "theirs" is the same misuse in
  miniature [CF:WILLING-STEP]; using the method to obtain research consent is ruled out by name.
- **Permission as ritual.** Asking "may I share an idea?" when you were going to say it anyway is the
  named misuse of [CF:ASK-PERMISSION] — it converts a safeguard into a rhetorical device.
- **Tone inverts every technique.** Autonomy statements said grudgingly communicate one-up judgment;
  amplified reflection with sarcasm becomes mockery; coming alongside as a bluff becomes reverse
  psychology; overstated control claims ("you can't drink") say something untrue and invite the test
  you fear [CF:INTENSITY-DIAL], [CF:COMING-ALONGSIDE], [CF:AUTONOMY-EMPHASIS].
- **Prosecutor summaries.** Collecting someone's own statements as evidence backfires; all-problem
  summaries demoralize; a summary that ends on the status quo leaves the status quo ringing [CF:BOUQUET].
- **Affirming the wrong content.** People produce more of whatever gets affirmed, including content
  that leads somewhere worse [CF:AFFIRM].
- **Rapid-fire single suggestions.** They manufacture sustain talk, and the refusals then get misread
  as denial or difficulty — a helper artifact reattributed to the person's character [CF:OPTION-MENU].
- **Adverse effects at high initial readiness.** The sharpest contraindication the tradition owns:
  someone already decided can be talked backwards by being asked to weigh it again [CF:SKIPEVOKE].
- **Deep listening opens material the method cannot hold.** The book's own demonstrations reach a
  disclosure of partner violence after about seven minutes and a childhood bereavement behind an
  out-of-awareness motive. There is no protocol here for what surfaces. That is the boundary that
  matters most for a lens sitting beside a live therapist [CF:ADMITLIMIT], [CF:VERTICAL-AMB],
  [CF:EXPERIENCING-SHIFT] — and a risk signal ends the frame entirely, per safety-protocol §1.
- **Directionality is handed back unresolved.** The authors list contested cases — vaccination, organ
  donation, end-of-life choices, harm reduction, pregnancy counselling, teen contraception,
  interrogation — and offer four guidelines rather than a decision rule. The bigger your stake in
  someone's choice, the further you must move toward declared neutrality; where your stake crosses
  the line, the method cannot tell you.
