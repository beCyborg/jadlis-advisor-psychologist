# advisor-compassion — myths and demotions

What this tradition itself debunks, which of its numbers did not survive verification, which framings to refuse, and how it fails when applied naively. Nothing here is optional politeness: the lens's single best-documented harm case is created by applying its own core exercise to the people it was designed for.

## 1. Myths the tradition debunks about itself

- **"Self-compassion is weakness, self-indulgence, lowered standards."** The near-universal objection, and it rests on self-dislike rather than argument. Compassionate self-correction keeps the same improvement aims; only the emotion delivering them changes ([CFT:SELFCORR]).
- **"Compassion means being nice."** It is not people-pleasing, hiding feelings, submission, apologising by default or absorbing responsibility that is not yours. Kindness performed to win approval — submissive compassion — is a distinct motivation that predicts feeling unappreciated ([CFT:FLOW-OUT]).
- **"Compassion means never feeling anger."** It starts with honesty about what is actually felt; "I'm not an angry person" commonly marks feared, unprocessed rage, and impotent rage maintains low mood ([CFT:EMO-ENGAGE]).
- **"Self-compassionate behaviour = pampering."** It is what flourishing requires, which includes hard confrontations and bearing the guilt that follows them ([CFT:ASSERT]).
- **"Soothing yourself is self-compassion."** The bath-wine-film instead of the feared event maintains the difficulty; avoidance survives on the relief cycle. Being the world's best avoider of dogs never removes the dog fear ([CFT:APPROACH]).
- **"Compassionate thinking is positive thinking."** The target is validation plus balance connected to felt warmth; thoughts are events rather than facts, and attention rebalancing is bias correction, not optimism ([CFT:THREC], [CFT:ATT-FUNC]).
- **"Mindfulness means clearing the mind."** Noticing the wander is the repetition; frequent falling asleep usually means sleep debt, not failure ([CFT:MINDFUL]).
- **"The exercise works when I feel soothed."** Breathing's deliverable is physiological slowing and groundedness; chasing the feeling backfires ([CFT:SBR]).
- **"Imagery must be vivid."** Fleeting, hazy or non-visual imagery is normal and sufficient; intention outweighs picture quality ([CFT:SAFE-PLACE], [CFT:COMP-SELF]).
- **"The goal is deleting the critic / becoming shameless."** Both are wrong targets: the critic has protective aims and attacking it fires threat, and brief shame has prosocial functions. The target is conversion, and guilt must remain learnable and bearable ([CFT:CRIT-WORK], [CFT:SHAME-WORK]).
- **"Nothing bad enough happened to me to justify this."** Difficulties are valid without dramatic history; suppression rebounds and emotional hoarding erupts.
- **"Compassion should feel natural."** Without lived models it feels alien and remains learnable — but if you believe compassion is weakness, more practice hours will not help until the belief is worked ([CFT:FBR-WORK]).
- **"Rational disputation will fix this."** The founding observation of the lens: evidence-for-and-against bounces off an angry-dominant critic; alternatives help only when they can be felt as reassuring ([CFT:WARMREAD]).
- **"It didn't work when I tried it in a crisis."** Crisis-only practice guarantees false negatives, and imagery fragments bolted onto another model underperform — the canon warns practitioners about this directly.
- **"Self-compassion is one construct."** The popular self-compassion construct is not the CFT model, and many self-criticism scales measure adjacent things — social comparison, low self-esteem.

## 2. Numbers and claims demoted or refuted in verification

Use only the post-correction form. These override anything the book digests state more confidently.

- **CFT/self-compassion for self-criticism: MODERATE → LOW.** The pooled g=0.51 (Wakelin 2021) is driven by passive controls. Say: better than nothing; superiority over active treatment is not shown.
- **Grief-brand superiority: demoted and retracted.** Hao 2024 (55 studies, N=10,330) shows five modalities with overlapping intervals. Say: grief work has effect at elevated prolonged-grief symptoms; there is no winning brand, and at ordinary grief the effect is near zero and potentially harmful ([CFT:GRIEF-WORK]).
- **"Multiple meta-analyses agree" is downweighted** wherever author groups overlap, and developer-affiliated trials inflate effects by roughly 30%. This bites here: nearly the entire CFT evidence base is authored by the model's originators.
- **Small-trial advantages are artifacts.** Publication-bias testing in the depression literature is strongly positive (Egger p<0.001), and this lens's quantitative wins are pilots, uncontrolled series and waitlist comparisons.
- **Indirectness caps everything twice over.** No study tested a clinician-free format, and no study tested the self-administered workbook. Procedures transfer; effect sizes do not.
- **"Expressive writing is unsafe by default" is bounded** — newer meta-analysis finds a small positive effect. Only unstructured venting during active rumination-driven distress is contraindicated ([CFT:LETTER]).
- **Never reuse the parts-work folk rule** that solo work is safe with "protectors present" and crashes at two to three weeks — refuted as anecdote-derived pseudo-precision. The boundary is by operation plus a state gate ([CFT:MULTISELF], [CFT:BASEFIRST]).
- **Do not lean on process numbers as though they were outcomes:** alliance r=.278 is an association (its upgrade was invalidated), homework presence d=0.48 is LOW, and there is no reliable practice-completion benchmark to hold anyone to.
- **Unguided is weaker than every guided format** (HIGH) — including this lens's own self-help format.
- **Facial feedback is weakly replicated**, the mother's-voice oxytocin study is used analogically, and "imagery beats words for emotion" is stated more confidently than its sources support ([CFT:SBR]).

## 3. Anti-recommendations — framings to refuse and why

- **Do not present CFT as the method to choose for self-criticism.** Target→method arrows are working hypotheses for composing a lens set, never treatment assignment; roughly half rest on LOW evidence, and this one specifically was demoted.
- **Do not treat the workbook as a standalone treatment above the subclinical threshold.** It offers no severity guidance, no cut-offs even for its own questionnaire, and nothing on when a workbook is insufficient. Above that threshold a human in the loop is mandatory — for adherence and deterioration monitoring, not for symptom gain.
- **Do not equate the popular self-compassion construct with this model** when quoting evidence in either direction; they are measured differently.
- **Do not use the three circles as an identity or a diagnosis.** The exercise exists to move a person off "I am broken" and onto system balance; used the other way it manufactures a new label.
- **Do not run compassion imagery bolted onto an unrelated frame,** or as a relaxation preliminary before hard material — the canon says explicitly that this underperforms.
- **Do not import the competitive-culture critique into a personal verdict.** It is cultural argument, not data, and it displaces the person's actual situation.
- **Do not offer the lens where the threat system is responding accurately.** Ongoing abuse, poverty, discrimination or a workplace that keeps threat justified get one assertiveness protocol and little else here; treating an accurate threat response as an attention bias is the tradition's structural blind spot.

## 4. Documented failure modes and iatrogenic patterns

- **Compassion practice firing threat in high self-critics.** The best-documented harm in the lens: lowered heart-rate variability under compassion imagery in exactly the people it targets (Rockliff et al. 2008). Pushing more practice is the documented failure mode; the fear is the target ([CFT:FBR-WORK]). The canon documents the failure far better than any fix — there is no protocolized remedy.
- **Befriending a critic that is an internalized abuser** is a category error requiring a different protocol; the screening gate is mandatory before any critic work ([CFT:COMP-CHAIR]).
- **Experiential techniques overwhelm a substantial minority even inside therapy** — immediate post-session overwhelm was recorded in 15 of 29 chair-work and 17 of 21 imagery-rescripting sessions, with long-term outcomes still positive. Solo, that overwhelm has no one holding it.
- **The trauma boundary is self-policed.** Both books route trauma material out and then leave detection of flooding and dissociation to the reader mid-exercise, which is when self-assessment is least reliable. The lens enforces the routing rather than assuming it.
- **Deep solo work has a cost curve.** Plan it before rest days with a 1–2 day recovery buffer; stop and route to a session on flooding lasting beyond a day, functional decline, intrusive images, flashbacks, dissociation, panic, worsening sleep or suicidal thoughts. Writing stop-markers: past 45 minutes with no resolution, closing tenser than opening, the same topic with no shift, anxiety on skipping.
- **Base rates for the format itself.** Negative effects appear in 14–92% of self-help interventions and roughly 18% of self-help books carry harmful advice; unguided CBT is no better than usual care though not harmful in individual-patient data (5.8% vs 9.1% deterioration). Temporary worsening is baseline, not a switch signal.
- **Programme load and drop-out.** Twelve competencies and dozens of practices over weeks; the authors acknowledge the overwhelm risk and answer only with "small steps". No minimal effective subset exists, so the lens must hand over at most a few actions at a time, and a skipped practice is a badly designed practice, never a character finding.
- **Validation that curdles.** Validation followed by a reversal, or used to pressure a feeling into changing, reproduces the invalidation it was meant to repair; validate the feeling, not every behaviour ([CFT:EMO-ENGAGE]).
- **Compassion practice awakening grief.** Common, expected, and out of solo scope past naming it. No reframe, no silver lining, no growth narrative belongs anywhere near it ([CFT:GRIEF-WORK]).
- **In an AI-mediated format specifically:** repeat reassurance on the same worry is a compulsion being fed rather than a question being answered; endless availability without a closing ritual is a documented harm mode; and agreeing with the person's own framing is this format's most-documented failure — the lens is required to say when it reads the situation differently.
- **Cultural flatness.** Secularized Western framing throughout, with contemplative practices borrowed and no discussion of readers for whom they carry religious or cultural charge.
- **The lens talking someone out of leaving.** Because it reframes protective strategies as understandable and treats warmth as a training target, it can read a decision to withdraw from a harmful situation as avoidance to be graded. Where the environment is the problem, compassion here means boundaries, refusal and exit — not tolerance practice.
- **Measurement is thin underneath the whole structure.** By the canon's own account: self-criticism scales measuring adjacent constructs, no agreed cross-language definition of compassion, fear-of-compassion measures still in development at publication. Quoting a scale score as evidence of change overstates what the instruments carry.

## 5. One-line summary for the synthesizer

The lens is strong at explaining *why* the person's own reasoning bounces off, weak at proving its remedy beats any active alternative, and it has one known way of making things worse — pushing compassion practice on someone whose body answers it with threat.
