# advisor-emotion-regulation — core principles, ranked by evidence

## How this file ranks things

Two indirectness layers sit above every claim below and cannot be argued away. First, population: the manual was built for chronically suicidal, high-severity clients in a specialized program; the closest trial to an ordinary functioning adult is Neacsiu 2014 (adults with anxiety plus emotion dysregulation and no personality-disorder diagnosis). Second, delivery: the entire trial base used facilitated groups with homework review and phone coaching. The author states outright that there is no research on this manual as self-study. Solo workbook use — this council's actual mode — is the untested delta, and no study anywhere tested a clinician-free format.

So the honest ranking is not "DBT works". It is: a few operations inside DBT have their own evidence from outside DBT; the skills bundle has package-level evidence in facilitated formats; the connective theory has none; and our delivery mode has none at all.

## Tier 1 — operations with independent support outside DBT

### Direct physiological down-regulation — [ER:TIP]
The dive response (cold on the face, breath held, onset in 15–30 seconds) and slow-paced breathing at roughly 5–6 breaths per minute with a longer exhale are established physiology, not DBT findings. Paired muscle relaxation descends from Smith 1980. This is the only place in the lens where the mechanism is measurable in seconds and does not depend on the person believing anything. It is also the only thing that reliably works above the breakdown point.

### Operant learning and functional analysis — [ER:CHAIN], [ER:ADDICT]
Reinforcement, extinction bursts, intermittent reinforcement of escalation, and functional analysis of a behavior chain are the backbone of behavior therapies with a century of independent work behind them. Practical consequence for the lens: a behavior that persists is being maintained by something, and the maintaining consequence is the target — not the person's character. The Community Reinforcement elements in the addiction move (Meyers & Squires 2001) carry their own trials; dialectical abstinence does not.

### Exposure lineage — [ER:OPPACT-FEAR]
Repeated approach to avoided situations is the best-evidenced change operation in the whole manual, with graded exposure holding a MODERATE-level guideline standard (PG26 Р4). This lens still tiers it REQUIRES-THERAPIST, because the evidence attaches to a designed, graded, prolonged, repeated hierarchy — and a self-run approach that fails reinforces the fear. Strong evidence for the operation is not evidence for the solo version of the operation.

### Behavioral activation lineage — [ER:ABCPLEASE], [ER:OPPACT] (sadness variant)
Scheduling positive activity and acting opposite to withdrawal is behavioral activation, which stands as a standalone component at MODERATE for depression (PG26 Р1: BA and cognitive restructuring both hold up when pulled out of the package). The mastery and values components ride along without their own trials.

### Cognitive appraisal checking — [ER:CHECKFACTS]
The fact-checking sequence is the cognitive-restructuring operation under another name, and cognitive restructuring is MODERATE as a standalone component. Note the limit the manual itself insists on: appraisal work cannot move an emotion that already fits the facts, and pushing it there converts a skill into self-invalidation.

### Problem solving — [ER:PROBSLV]
Standard problem-solving-therapy structure, imported whole. Evidence is method-level from that tradition, not from DBT trials.

## Tier 2 — package-level: the skills bundle, tested as a bundle

### The skills module as a unit
Linehan's 2015 component analysis found skills plus case management performing close to the full program, which is the strongest statement available that the skills carry weight independent of individual DBT. Neacsiu 2014 is the RCT closest to a non-clinical adult. Against the strongest comparator, a specialized program held its own in a head-to-head with schema therapy (n=204), but that trial was a superiority design and p=0.27 with d=0.15 is not a demonstration of equivalence, and dropout was around half in both arms.

The critical demotion this lens must carry: **DBT skills as a standalone intervention are rated LOW** (PG26 Р1). The routing arrow "difficulty tolerating and leaving situations → DBT skills" is a working hypothesis for lens selection, not a treatment assignment (PG26 ROUTING-AS-HYPOTHESES).

### What sits at package level and nothing more
Each of these was delivered inside a tested bundle and none has been isolated:

- naming and modelling an emotion [ER:NAME], routing it [ER:ROUTE], riding it out [ER:WAVE];
- crisis survival in place [ER:CRISISMENU], the urge gap [ER:STOP], arousal gating itself [ER:BRKPOINT];
- acceptance work on ordinary unchangeable facts [ER:RADACC] and imaginal rehearsal [ER:COPEAHEAD];
- the interpersonal set — scripted requests [ER:DEARMAN], the relationship and self-respect rules [ER:GIVEFAST], validation [ER:VALIDATE];
- the failure checklist [ER:TROUBLESHOOT].

There are no effect sizes for any individual skill, no dosing data, and no ranking of which skills carry the outcome variance — the manual presents hundreds of techniques flat, on the claim that every skill works for someone and no skill works for everyone. Selection burden therefore falls on the user, which is itself a documented overwhelm risk.

### Borrowed elements with method-level support elsewhere
Defusion techniques [ER:MOT] are shared with ACT; mindfulness practice [ER:MINDCORE] with MBCT adaptations (Segal et al. 2013); imagery rehearsal for nightmares [ER:NIGHTMARE-RESCRIPT] and stimulus control for sleep [ER:SLEEP-HYGIENE] with the insomnia literature; urge surfing with relapse prevention. Method-level support for a technique elsewhere is weaker than a trial of it here, and this lens should say which of the two it is holding.

## Tier 3 — internally coherent, empirically unmeasured

These are the load-bearing beams of the model with no independent test:

- **Emotion as a full-system response.** Prompting event → interpretation → body → action urge → expression → aftereffects, with every skill entering at one component and changing the whole. This is the lens's single most useful frame and it is a model, not a finding.
- **Arousal gating and the breakdown point** [ER:BRKPOINT]. The claim that complicated skills become physiologically unusable above a personal threshold, and that the fixed order is crisis survival → mindfulness of the emotion → regulation. Clinically compelling, formally untested.
- **The four options for any painful problem** — solve it, change the response, accept it, or stay miserable — with a skill set behind each. Organizing device, not evidence.
- **Wise Mind** as a third state synthesizing reason and emotion. Unfalsifiable as stated; useful as a stopping rule before acting.
- **Dialectics and the middle path** — kernel of truth on the other side, multi-causation instead of blame. Adapted from Miller, Rathus & Linehan 2007.
- **The six validation levels and the ten-factor intensity ladder.** Detailed, teachable, and unmeasured; the intensity ladder in particular carries the useful anti-claim that maximal assertiveness is not the right default.
- **Biosocial theory** — sensitivity times invalidating environment, maintained transactionally. An etiological story that the skills do not depend on.

## Tier 4 — extrapolation to this council's format

Everything above degrades further when it passes through a solo, AI-mediated channel:

- Unguided self-help is weaker than every guided format and no better than usual care, though an individual-patient-data analysis found it not harmful on deterioration (5.8% versus 9.1%). Lay guidance closes most of the gap — which is an argument for the live therapist beside this council, not for the council alone.
- Negative effects are reported in 14–92% of self-help interventions depending on definition, and roughly 18% of self-help books were found to contain harmful advice. Volume is a failure mode here: 225 pleasant events and dozens of worksheets presume group accountability.
- Presence of homework is a d=0.48 effect rated LOW, compliance correlates r=.22–.27, and quality beats quantity (g=1.07 versus 0.51). There is no reliable completion benchmark, so this lens judges the quality of one practice, never a completion percentage.
- App retention collapses from 69.4% on day one to 3.3% by day thirty; the formats that survive are one step, under five minutes, and forgiving of skipped days. A worksheet-heavy plan is a plan that will be abandoned in week two.
- Above a moderate symptom threshold (PHQ-9 above 9) a human in the loop is mandatory — for adherence and deterioration monitoring, not because the human adds symptom points.

## Numbers this lens may quote, and in what form

- Skills plus case management performing close to the full program: Linehan 2015 component analysis — package-level, no per-skill breakdown.
- Head-to-head against schema therapy: n=204, p=0.27, d=0.15, dropout 52% versus 46% — say "no difference shown in a superiority design", never "equivalent".
- DBT skills as a standalone: LOW (PG26 Р1). Say "weaker evidence when pulled out of the program", not "unproven" and not "proven".
- Unguided self-help deterioration: 5.8% versus 9.1% in an individual-patient-data analysis — the harm claim is what is absent, not the benefit claim.
- Homework: presence d=0.48 (LOW), compliance r=.22–.27, quality versus quantity g=1.07 versus 0.51; no completion benchmark exists.
- App retention: 69.4% day one → 3.3% day thirty; formats that survive are one step, five minutes, skip-forgiving.
- Human-in-the-loop threshold: PHQ-9 above 9; justified by adherence and deterioration monitoring, not by symptom gain.
- Adverse events in psychotherapy generally: 5–20%; deterioration 4–5% versus 12–13% in controls; therapists caught 3 of 40 deteriorations — the therapist beside this council is not a safety guarantee either.
- Physiological anchors that may be stated plainly: dive response onset 15–30 seconds, water not below 50°F, paced breathing 5–6 per minute, sleep 7–9 hours, movement up to 20 minutes daily, naps 10 minutes or less.
- Number that must never be reused: the claimed elevated self-harm risk from online DBT skills (a hazard ratio of 1.29) was refuted as outcome substitution (PG26 Р2).

## Cross-cutting principles this lens argues from

1. **Change any component and the whole response shifts** — so the first question is never "what should I feel" but "which component is reachable right now".
2. **Arousal decides which skills exist.** Above the breakdown point, body chemistry first; a cognitive move offered there is a move that will fail and then be read as personal failure.
3. **Facts before change.** Whether the emotion fits the facts and whether acting on it is effective are two separate questions, and they route to different skills [ER:ROUTE].
4. **Acceptance and change are not opposites**; the skill is choosing the right one for what is actually changeable, and re-choosing it every time you drift [ER:RADACC].
5. **A skill failure is data, not a verdict.** The checklist exists because the manual explicitly denies that correct execution always works [ER:TROUBLESHOOT]; an environment more powerful than the skill is a legitimate answer.
6. **Emotion intensity is a signal, not evidence about the world.** This is the anti-claim the whole tradition rests on, and it cuts both ways: it also means low intensity is not evidence that nothing happened.
