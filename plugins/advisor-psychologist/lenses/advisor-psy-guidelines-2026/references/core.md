# advisor-psy-guidelines-2026 — core

Not "the method works". This lens has no method: it ranks what the surrounding literature actually supports, at what magnitude, and where the council is extrapolating. Levels are GRADE-like (HIGH / MODERATE / LOW / VERY LOW), origin report in parentheses. Every number here is already in post-correction form — see `myths-and-demotions.md` for what it replaced.

## Meta-rules that outrank any individual finding

- **Two indirectness layers tax everything** [PG26:INDIRECTNESS-TAX]. Clinical→subclinical population, and live-therapist→AI delivery. No study tested a clinician-free format. Procedures transfer; effect sizes do not. Every solo application operates outside the evidence base (Р1/Р3).
- **Small-study advantage is an artifact** [PG26:ALLEGIANCE-DISCOUNT]. Egger p<0.001 across the depression literature; developer-affiliated trials inflate by roughly 30% (allegiance r=.262) (Р1/Р3).
- **"Several meta-analyses agree" is not replication** when the author groups overlap, or when the systems tested are wrappers over the same two-to-four base models (Р1/Р5).
- **VERY LOW means unmeasured, not refuted.** The refuted items live in the demotions file, not here (Р1/Р2).
- **The source GRADE table outranks report prose; the part-digest outranks this file** (Р1 integrity note).

## HIGH — usable as constraints, not as effect promises

### Author-issued contraindications for experiential work
Chair work with vulnerable material is contraindicated by its own authors at risk of retraumatization, self-harm or suicidality; early trauma-memory rescripting is performed by the therapist even inside therapy (Arntz 2025). These are protocol constraints, not measured effects — which is exactly why they are not negotiable (Р2). [PG26:STATE-GATE] [PG26:IMG-NONTRAUMA] [PG26:EXPOSURE-THERAPIST]

### Guided beats unguided, in every comparison available
Unguided self-help is weaker than every guided format and no better than usual care; lay guidance closes most of the gap (Р2/Р3). [PG26:SELFHELP-VETTING]

### Normative timelines
Review at 2–4 weeks; a zero-response checkpoint at 4–6 weeks with a structured checklist before any change of approach; at least four extra sessions of relapse prevention after a successful course. "6–12 sessions" is a conversation trigger, not a threshold (Р2/Р3). [PG26:SWITCH-CHECK] [PG26:RELAPSE-4S] [PG26:EARLY-RESPONSE]

### Real-world engagement collapse — day-30 retention 3.3%
Day 1 at 69.4%, day 30 at 3.3%; trial engagement runs about four times real-world. Mood-diary adherence around 64%. Formats that survive are one step, five minutes, and forgiving of skips (Р4). [PG26:HONEST-CEILING] [PG26:MICRO-TASK]

### US statutory and professional facts
Bans on AI diagnosis, medication advice, treatment-plan change and mental-state attribution (Illinois); exemptions confirmed in Illinois, Nevada, Rhode Island; Utah disclosure mandate; at least seven states restricting by 2026-08; fines $10k (IL) / $15k (NV). Professional-body survey 2026: 77% of psychologists hear of patient AI use (6.3% completion caveat) (Р4/Р5). [PG26:NO-DIAG-MED] [PG26:POSITION-SELFHELP] [PG26:JURIS-BOUND]

## MODERATE — the load-bearing tier

### Bona fide psychotherapy vs waitlist, low mood: d=−0.62…−0.92
The one large effect in this whole file. Response 41% vs 17% — meaning most people do not respond within about two months (Р1/Р3/Р4). [PG26:EXPECT-BASE]

### Between-method differences ≈ zero — for depression only: d=0.01…−0.30
Barth 2013, 198 RCTs / N=15,118; the sole significant contrast vanishes in large trials. Does NOT transfer to trauma or chronic-worry targets, to ritual-like targets, or to acceptance-based and schema lenses (absent from the network) (Р1). [PG26:TARGET-NOT-BRAND] [PG26:PROTOCOL-TARGETS]

### Behavioural activation and cognitive restructuring as standalone components
Each works on its own; the interpersonal lens for low mood likewise MODERATE. "Parts as good as the whole" (Bell 2013) is the correct dismantling statement (Р1). [PG26:ROUTING-AS-HYPOTHESES]

### Adherence-to-outcome null — the best-evidenced claim in the alliance literature
Pre-registered meta-analysis: protocol adherence does not predict outcome; competence r=0.17; therapist effects ≈5%; school-versus-school p=0.39 (Р3). [PG26:JUDGE-PROCESS]

### Twice-weekly in the acute phase: d=0.55, dropout 16 vs 32
Same total sessions, faster acute response, roughly half the dropout, HR 1.48 — and no advantage at 12–24 months (Р3). [PG26:DOSE-DENSITY]

### Dropout 19.7% weighted, and structure is the lever
17.8% time-limited vs 29.0% not; 18.3% manualized vs 28.3% not. Strongest early predictor is a missed session inside the first four (Р3/Р4). [PG26:RETENTION-STRUCTURE]

### Processes beat techniques: r=.291/.267 vs .102
Setup quality of a between-session task beats checking whether it was done. Graded, repeated, prolonged exposure is the guideline standard; single-session debriefing of fresh trauma carries a harm signal around OR 2.5 (Cochrane) (Р4). [PG26:MICRO-TASK] [PG26:EXPOSURE-THERAPIST] [PG26:NO-FRESH-DEBRIEF]

### Adverse events 5–20%; deterioration 4–5% vs 12–13% control; clinicians catch ~3%
Corrected NNT against deterioration 9.7 (care as usual) / 5.7 (waitlist). About 58% report unpleasant memories surfacing. Therapists caught 3 of 40 deteriorations — the entire case for measurement (Р2/Р3). [PG26:EXPECT-BASE] [PG26:TRIAD-ROM]

### Chatbots vs inactive controls, 4–8 weeks: g≈0.26–0.31, NNT 11–12
Publication-bias-clean for efficacy. Peak around week 8, gone at three months (Р5). [PG26:HONEST-CEILING]

### Guided over unguided for adherence: g≈0.26–0.29
This — plus response 48% vs 37% at PHQ-9>9 — is the justification for a human in the loop. Not symptom gain: guided-versus-unguided symptom difference is MD −0.8 PHQ-9, below the minimal clinically important difference of 1.7–5 (Р5). [PG26:ROUTE-HUMAN]

### Grief work only at elevated prolonged-grief symptoms
The entry condition is measured, not assumed; in uncomplicated grief the intervention effect is near zero and can do harm (Р1/Р2). No brand wins: Hao 2024 NMA, 55 studies / N=10,330, five significant modalities with overlapping CIs. [PG26:GRIEF-GATE]

### Loneliness interventions — Hickin 2021, 31 RCTs / N=3,959
The effect is MODERATE; the social-cognition mechanism story is LOW and belongs to an older meta-analysis (Р1).

### Schema-focused work in the personality-pattern trials (avoidant/dependent range): pooled g=0.359 (CI 0.006–0.711)
Barely clears zero, and neither the group arm (g=0.859) nor the individual arm (g=0.163) is significant on its own. A small fragile pooled effect; the "group works, individual does not" story is unproven (Р1). [PG26:ALLEGIANCE-DISCOUNT]

## LOW — direction usable, magnitude not

### Alliance r=.278 (~8% of variance)
An association, not a lever, and not commensurable with randomized method contrasts. Weak alliance predicts dropout (d=.55) — that is its usable edge (Р3). [PG26:JUDGE-PROCESS]

### Homework presence d=0.48; quality over quantity g=1.07 vs 0.51; compliance r=.22–.27
No reliable completion benchmark exists anywhere in this literature (Р3/Р4). [PG26:MICRO-TASK]

### Routine outcome monitoring d≈0.13–0.27
Six independent meta-analyses. The off-track subgroup sits at the lower boundary — independent d=0.21 n.s., zero with a fully independent scale, partly regression to the mean. Deterioration prevention specifically is VERY LOW (feedback→deterioration OR 1.16, p=0.07). Sudden gains occur in 14–62% of patients (g=0.62–0.72) but are only visible with per-session measurement (Р3). [PG26:TRIAD-ROM]

### Rupture-repair r=.29
Repair training alone did not lift outcomes. Rupture markers are worth surfacing; the repair itself is therapist work (Р3). [PG26:SWITCH-CHECK]

### Compassion-focused work for self-criticism: g=0.51, passive-control-driven
Better than nothing; superiority over active treatment is not shown (Р1). [PG26:ROUTING-AS-HYPOTHESES]

### Skills-only formats and school-superiority claims
Standalone skills training LOW; schema-focused ≥ cognitive-behavioural LOW; perfectionism-specific cognitive work MODERATE for the target but untested against generic cognitive work; intolerance-of-uncertainty experiments LOW (N=60, waitlist, developer-authored) (Р1). [PG26:ROUTING-AS-HYPOTHESES]

### Skills-group equivalence in the BOOTS trial population
BOOTS is HIGH with caveats — superiority design, motivated cohort, n=204, p=0.27, d=0.15. p=0.27 is not equivalence (Р2). [PG26:ALLEGIANCE-DISCOUNT]

### AI as adjunct to live work: d≈0.56
Trials at eight weeks or under, young mild-to-moderate samples. Chatbots versus active controls: g=−0.08, phrased "effect not shown", not "no effect" (n=8, no equivalence margin). Anxiety outcomes LOW. Crisis-failure and sycophancy findings demoted to LOW (Р4/Р5). [PG26:HONEST-CEILING] [PG26:AI-ANTIPAT]

### All community-derived process heuristics
Sequencing, entry by cognitive style, recovery buffers, stop-markers, red-flag tiers, AI anti-patterns. These are failure modes, never prevalence. Loud testimonials are phase-fit artifacts (Р2/Р4/Р5). [PG26:SEQ-FIRST] [PG26:STOP-RULES] [PG26:FLAG-TIERS]

## VERY LOW — unmeasured; usable only as design intent

- **Personalized-matching algorithms.** No prospective validation (Р1). [PG26:NO-ALGO-MATCHING]
- **Transdiagnostic intolerance-of-uncertainty work.** N=37, completer-only (Р1).
- **Schema-focused work on subclinical patterns.** No direct trials (Р1).
- **The generative-model subgroup inside chatbot meta-analyses.** Small, heterogeneous, era-mixed (Р5).
- **ROM as deterioration prevention** (Р3). [PG26:TRIAD-ROM]
- **AI-induced psychosis** as a causal claim (Р5).
- **Adverse-event collection payoff and external risk classifiers.** Sensible design; zero-shot high-risk sensitivity 0.05–0.06 (Р5). [PG26:SAFETY-DESIGN]
- **Transfer of the US regulatory layer to the EU.** Absent from the evidence base entirely (Р5). [PG26:JURIS-BOUND]

## Explicit extrapolation — say so when using it

- The entire tier boundary between SELF and REQUIRES-THERAPIST is an operation-level judgment built from author contraindications plus harm signals, not from trials comparing solo and guided delivery of each operation (Р2). [PG26:STATE-GATE] [PG26:ERP-NO-ESC]
- The move set of this lens is normative scaffolding: mostly LOW-evidence process consensus, chosen because the safety data do not exist, not because the moves were measured. The correct sentence about the format is "safety is not established" — active adverse-event collection ran in 3 of 22 RCTs and 42 of 51 completed trials went unreported (Р5). [PG26:SAFETY-DESIGN]
- Scale scores (PHQ-9, GAD-7, and the rest) are surrogates for behaviour change; hard outcomes are almost never measured in the AI layer (Р5). <!-- privacy-ok: GAD-7 — публичная шкала из книги-источника, не данные пользователя -->
