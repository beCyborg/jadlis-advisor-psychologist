# advisor-schema — principles, tier-ranked by evidence

The tiers below rank *components*, not the school. Nothing here says "schema work works." Each tier says what is shown, in whom, at what magnitude, and where the line into extrapolation runs. Numbers are stated only in the post-correction form fixed by the calibration lens.

---

## Tier 1 — Borrowed components with independent trial support (the method does not own them)

These carry evidence from outside the tradition. When a move in this lens works, this is the most likely reason.

**Behavioural experiments, graded rehearsal, contingency management.** Broad CBT support as components. This is what actually carries [ST:BREAK], the behavioural half of [ST:CARD-DIARY], and the practice ladder inside [ST:PAUSE]. The schema framing supplies the *target selection*, not the mechanism.

**Decisional balance.** Standard CBT component; the whole of [ST:CM-PROCON] is a decisional balance with a schema-shaped label on the columns.

**Cognitive restructuring as a standalone component: MODERATE.** Supports the counter-evidence half of [ST:PM-SORT] and the ledger inside [ST:CARD-DIARY] — but as generic restructuring, not as anything schema-specific.

**Distress tolerance and mindfulness skills.** Their own trial base (Linehan 1993). [ST:SAFE-PLACE] and the grounding half of [ST:PAUSE] borrow them wholesale; placement inside this method is clinical judgement, not a finding.

**Caveat that applies to all of Tier 1:** component support does not transfer to *this delivery*. No study tested a clinician-free delivery of any of it. Procedures transfer; effect sizes do not.

---

## Tier 2 — The package, in personality-disorder samples: small, fragile, format unproven (pooled g = 0.359)

**Zhang 2023 meta-analysis, schema therapy for cluster-C patterns: pooled g = 0.359, CI 0.006–0.711.** The interval barely clears zero. Group format g = 0.859 and individual format g = 0.163 are *both* non-significant, so the popular reading "group works, individual doesn't" is an over-reading of noise; the honest statement is a small fragile pooled effect with the format story unproven. The corpus is not purely cluster-C. Bamelis 2014 is the individual-format cluster-C trial to cite alongside it — always with magnitudes, never as a bare name.

**Superiority over CBT: LOW.** Not established.

**Against a dialectical-behavioural comparator for the borderline pattern (BOOTS): HIGH quality with caveats** — the design was a superiority test, the cohort motivated, and p = 0.27 is a failure to find a difference, not a demonstration of equivalence.

**What this tier licenses:** the tradition as a *map* for entrenched, lifelong, relationship-recurring patterns in people who already carry a heavy pattern load. It does not license claims of superiority, and it does not license the mechanism story.

## Tier 3 — The eighteen-theme list: psychometric support only, no outcome claim

The item list behind [ST:MAP18] is the one construct here with real measurement behind it. Factor-analytic work on the schema questionnaire (Schmidt 1995; Lee 1999) recovered a structure close to the published list, with internal consistencies roughly .83 to .96 and test–retest .50 to .82 in non-clinical samples; a patient sample yielded 15 factors accounting for 54% of variance. One proposed theme (social undesirability) failed to emerge and was folded into the shame theme — a demotion the tradition itself made.

Two limits. First, this is evidence that the *list describes covariation*, not that changing a score changes a life. Second, questionnaire scoring is deliberately out of scope for this lens: scores route to a separate metrics layer, and [ST:MAP18] runs as a descriptive sort.

## Tier 4 — Clinical taxonomy with no validating instrument (most of the apparatus)

Everything in this tier is a structured clinical hypothesis. It can be useful for organising a mess; it cannot be cited as a finding.

**The five core needs** [ST:NEEDS5] — footnoted by the authors themselves as theory-derived and empirically untested. The two-pole compression (connection versus self-assertion) is a later reformulation with no validating measure at all.

**Four origin types and the temperament dimensions** [ST:ORIGINS4] — hypotheses. The only external anchor is temperamental stability (Kagan, Reznick & Snidman 1988); there is no measure of innate temperament in the model, so "constitutional versus pattern-driven" is a judgement call every time.

**Three coping styles and the perpetuation cycle** [ST:CM-PROCON], [ST:CM-DETECT] — clinical taxonomy, with the published tables described as illustrative rather than data-derived. The one non-obvious and durable claim inside it: opposite behaviours can serve the same underlying theme, so behaviour never identifies the theme.

**The ten states** [ST:MODE-MAP], [ST:SIX-PROBE], [ST:DISPROPORTION] — the tradition's own canon concedes them as provisional. Their operational value is that they turn "something is wrong with my life" into an addressable list; their epistemic value is near zero.

**The conditional/unconditional split, the per-theme change-channel weighting, "schema chemistry"** [ST:MAP18], [ST:PARTNER] — clinical observation, no trial.

**The mode-cycle clash and the mentalization question set** [ST:CYCLE-CLASH] — conceptual borrowings from a later contextual reformulation, with no outcome data.

---

## Tier 5 — Explicitly flagged speculation (the mechanism story)

The claim that argument alone does not shift these patterns because the affective and somatic components sit in a fast, coarse, unconscious fear-memory system rests on a two-track model (LeDoux 1996) that the authors state has **not** been established for schemas. It is doing heavy lifting: it justifies why cognitive work underperforms, why imagery is preferred, and why [ST:CRITERIA] redefines success as regulation rather than erasure. Treat the *practice* as usable and the *explanation* as a hypothesis. If the mechanism is wrong, [ST:CRITERIA]'s "most of these never fully clear" is an unfalsifiable excuse rather than a realistic expectation.

---

## Tier 0 — Where this lens has no evidence at all: everyday, subclinical patterns

**Subclinical schema therapy: VERY LOW.** There are no direct trials on non-clinical or subclinical patterns. Everything this lens says about an ordinary person with an ordinary difficult life is extrapolation from personality-disorder samples downward — a direction of transfer nobody has tested.

**The self-help delivery has no data of its own.** The workbook this lens draws its worksheets from has no trial. The one directly trialled title in the tradition is the older popular book (Yasar 2024, n = 110), tested as a standalone course, not as the paced adjunct these worksheets assume.

**Interchangeability findings do not reach here.** The depression network showing bona fide therapies to be roughly equivalent does not include this method; the equivalence must not be extended to it, nor beyond depression.

---

## Operating principles that follow from the tiers

1. **Map before change.** Recognition and origin come first, change second; the sequence is cognitive plus emotional, with behaviour as the follow-on [ST:MODE-MAP], [ST:BREAK].
2. **Behaviour never identifies the pattern.** The same theme produces opposite behaviour across people and decades. Read the need, not the act [ST:CM-PROCON].
3. **Disproportion plus uncontrollability, or it is not a pattern.** The model explains any behaviour after the fact, so the false-positive guard has to be applied deliberately [ST:DISPROPORTION].
4. **Coping is weakened, not erased.** Every coping style has a function; aggressive protection needs a substitute built before the old one goes [ST:CM-PROCON].
5. **The tier is on the operation, not the school.** The same tradition supplies naming and mapping (SELF) and imagery on wounded material (REQUIRES-THERAPIST). Nothing that *opens* vulnerable or traumatic material is a solo move [ST:VC-BRIDGE], [ST:VC-RESCRIPT], [ST:PP-FIGHT], [ST:DP-BYPASS].
6. **Insight is not change, and calm is not progress.** A compliant, articulate, homework-doing account is compatible with the switched-off state running the whole time [ST:DP-BYPASS].
7. **Outside data outranks introspection for the fight-back styles.** They emit no internal distress signal; the signal arrives from other people [ST:CM-DETECT].
8. **Success is three falling curves, not disappearance** — and that definition must not be used to explain away months of nothing [ST:CRITERIA].
